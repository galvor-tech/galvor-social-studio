# Instagram reel playbook (saved 24 Nov 2026)

Source: Kallaway (@kallawaymarketing) course notes the marketing lead shared, plus a breakdown of his reel
"The ultimate scriptwriting format for short-form" (instagram.com/reel/DfzlY0XMeeN, posted 16 Nov 2026,
38.8 s, ~5.1K likes, ~4.7K comments driven by a "comment SCRIPT" lead magnet). His view counts are his own claims.

## The belief: content is data + psychology, not guessing
Data says which videos already worked; psychology predicts which should work again. Be a content scientist,
not an artist. Replace intuition with a repeatable loop: make, measure, change one thing, repeat.

## Lego Brick Framework: every reel is a stack of 11 bricks (develop in this order)
1. **Format** (the canvas): breakdown, scenario, hero's journey, list, myth vs fact, before/after…
2. **Idea** = **Topic** + **Seed** (the one-line premise) + **Substance** (facts, takes, examples that carry emotion)
3. **Hook**, in order of importance: **Text hook** (on screen) → **Visual hook** (first frame) → **Spoken hook**
4. **Script**: story structure, **CTA**, retention
5. **Edit**: **visual layout**, pacing, captions, signature **elements**

Keep most bricks, swap one, and it's a new reel. You don't need to be original: find brick combinations that
already work in our niche (D2C, creator marketing, ads, AI for marketers) and remix them; change 2-3 bricks, not all 11.
Use the bricks to diagnose a flop: which brick failed (hook? seed? layout?), fix that one next time.

## The 4 "cannot lose" conditions
1. **Post consistently**: at least 3 reels a week (ours: Mon/Wed/Fri), ideally 5-7.
2. **Get measurably better each rep**: every reel carries an improvement hypothesis ("testing a text-hook style",
   "switching layout to split screen"). Write it in the post notes; check it in Results a week later.
3. **Send on-target viewers to an aligned offer**: the CTA leads to something that matches the video's topic
   (a doc, checklist, newsletter issue, Scout demo). Views without a path to a lead are an expensive hobby.
4. **Never stop**: skills lock in around rep 50; most quit at 25.

## Script structure (from the reel)
1. **Hook** (0-3 s)
2. **Lock-in zone**: the 2-3 sentences right after the hook that tell the viewer what they'll get and why to stay
3. **Body block 1** → **Rehook 1** → **Body block 2** → **Rehook 2** → *(optional)* Body block 3
4. *(optional)* **Closing** line
5. **CTA with an aligned lead magnet** (the offer matches the topic), then Follow @galvor.ai

A rehook is a one-line jolt between blocks (a question, a twist, "but here's the part nobody checks") that
resets attention.

## What his reel does in the edit (copy the mechanics, not the look)
- 38.8 s, eight short beats; layout changes every 3-9 s.
- **Split screen**: top half a graphic that builds piece by piece as he speaks (the script outline fills in
  line by line, colour-coded per section); bottom half the speaker, medium close-up.
- **Cuts to a full-screen face** on the key lines (the "nobody talks about this" line, the claim, the CTA).
- A persistent **title bar** at the top (red label) so a muted viewer always knows the topic.
- **Captions**: 1-2 words at a time, bold sans, white with a soft shadow, centred.
- **Proof beat**: a grid of thumbnails of videos that used the structure ("works for any niche").
- **Sound**: low hip-hop bed under the voice; a pop/click on every graphic line, whooshes on transitions.
- **CTA**: "comment SCRIPT and I'll send you the doc", with page thumbnails of the doc on screen. The
  comment-for-doc offer is why comments (4.7K) nearly equal likes.

## How Galvor uses this
- Every reel script in the Studio states its bricks in the setup line: format, seed, text hook, visual hook,
  spoken hook, layout, CTA/lead magnet, and the **one brick being tested** (the improvement hypothesis).
- Default build for a teaching reel: split screen (graphic builds on top, speaker below), full-face cuts on the
  hook, the surprising line and the CTA; 1-2 word captions; a title bar; 25-40 s.
- Aligned lead magnets we can offer: the Decoded issue on the same topic ("link in bio"), a one-page checklist,
  a Scout sample report. A "comment KEYWORD for the doc" CTA needs Instagram DM automation, which isn't set up
  yet; until it is, use "link in bio" and keep "Follow @galvor.ai" as the close.
- Remix, don't invent: before writing, name 2-3 reels in our niche that worked and which bricks we're keeping.
