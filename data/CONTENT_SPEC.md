# Galvor Social Studio — Content Spec & Brand Guide

Source of truth for every post (human- or AI-written). Derived from: galvor.ai, *Decoded by Galvor* Issue #3,
"Galvor LinkedIn Plan: 3 Voices, 150 Topics, 90 Days (v3)", LinkedIn Tracker & Current Metrics (Nov 2026).

## 1. What Galvor is
Creative marketing intelligence for D2C performance teams. Decodes why ads win or lose (50+ attributes per ad:
hook, cast, pacing, CTA, setting), prices each creative attribute against cost per order, keeps a **Brand Memory**,
and runs a fortnightly creative loop (Decode → Prove → Create → Run). "We price the attribute, not the ad."
"We never touch the media budget." Modules: Decode, Brand Memory, Run, Vira (a digital co-worker that shows its
thinking), Ask Galvor, Scripts, Generate, Actions (Scale / Cut / Shift), Galvor Scout (creator shortlisting).
Audience: Indian D2C founders, CMOs, heads of performance/growth, brand & creative managers, agencies
(~₹40L+/month on paid social, 20+ creatives a month). HQ Mumbai; followers mostly Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi.

**Core belief (every post comes back to it): Every ad should make the next one better.**
Phrases we repeat on purpose: "Every ad should make the next one better." · "Plenty of data, no memory." ·
"From dashboards to decisions." · "Scale, cut, refresh, test." · "Learn → create → measure → learn."

## 2. Voice & tone
Short declarative sentences. Confident, specific, slightly contrarian, never salesy. Numbers and process language.
Plain English (Indian-English spelling: colour, analyse, programme is fine). Mobile-first: 1–2 line paragraphs.
India-specific beats generic global martech: festive budgets, regional-language creative, WhatsApp-run creator work,
marketplaces, Indian D2C cost structures.

## 3. Rules for every post (lint enforces most of these)
1. One post, one argument. 2. Open with tension, an observation or a claim — never "Excited to share".
3. Start from something real (saw, measured, built, changed our mind). 4. Earn the product mention; problem first.
5. **Never claim a competitor's spend or ROAS from public data.** 6. **No client names or client numbers** without
written permission — anonymise ("a premium linen D2C brand"). 7. Small samples = "a signal"/"a pattern to test".
8. Three voices must sound different. 9. Short paragraphs. 10. Question at the end only if a real conversation exists.
11. Links go in the **first comment** on LinkedIn; "link in bio" on Instagram. 12. No markdown (**bold**) in captions.
13. Banned words: excited to share, thrilled, game-changer, revolutionise, unlock, unleash, delve, cutting-edge,
seamlessly, elevate, next-level, skyrocket, synergy, "in today's fast-paced".
14. **Follow CTA = Galvor, always.** The closing call to action asks people to follow **Galvor** (LinkedIn) /
**@galvor.ai** (Instagram), never an individual, even on Sunil's, Raj's or Shrutik's own posts. Never write
"Follow Raj", "Follow me", "Follow for…" or "Follow along".
15. Asks: 70% of posts no ask, 20% soft ask ("comment 'brief' and I'll send it"), 10% direct ("we'll decode your category free").

## 4. Facts bank — the ONLY numbers allowed (never invent a statistic)
From *Decoded* #3 (Galvor analysis of every new video ad launched May–Sep 2026 across brands we work with):
- New ads that looked like many running ads got **36% less** first-month spend.
- New message over a familiar first shot: **16% less**. Proven message in a new look: **47% more**.
- Six or more look-alikes launched within three days each got **48% less** first-month spend.
- Some ad styles start ~**35% dearer per order** in their first two months and end close to average over their life.
- One brand's creative diversity score went **63 → 92** (out of 100) two months after Galvor's recommended changes.
- Meta (public statements, as cited in Decoded #3): Andromeda scores **over a billion ads a second**, **100×** the 2024
  system, **+2.4%** conversions on Facebook since 2025; GEM reads **twice** as long a history per person, about
  **19%** more conversions since last year; GenRecs shortens (doesn't remove) the learning phase. The "Creative
  diversity" column started appearing late August 2026 and reached most accounts in the last week of October.
From galvor.ai results (anonymised, publishable):
- Fashion (premium linen & cotton D2C): **+67% RoAS**, **+25% hold rate**, **$3,800** reallocated in month one.
- Beauty (clean skincare & serum D2C): **+112% RoAS**, **3× hook rate**, **30% less** wasted spend.
- Wellness (Ayurvedic supplements D2C): **+91% RoAS**, **+135% hold rate**, **2.6×** uplift vs aspirational content.
- A Run cycle example: **−18% CPO** (Cycle 14). Decode tags **50+ attributes** per ad. Loop runs **every two weeks**.
Every stat slide must carry a `source` line, e.g. "Source: Galvor analysis · ads launched May–Sep 2026" or
"Source: galvor.ai/results · anonymised client". If you need a number not listed here, don't use a number.

## 5. Platform conventions
**LinkedIn** — caption ≤3,000 chars (aim 700–1,300); first line ≤210 chars (it's the hook before "see more");
≤5 hashtags (use 3) at the end; link + CTA in `first_comment`. Personal-profile posts are written by that person
in first person. Formats: `text` (1 optional statement slide), `image` (1 slide), `carousel` (6–9 slides; becomes a
PDF document post), `poll` (question ≤140 chars, 2–4 options ≤30 chars each, duration "1 week"), `video`
(native, under 60s, `script` required, 1 cover slide with size `story`). Company page reshare posts: `text`.
**Instagram (@galvor.ai, voice `company` only)** — caption ≤2,200 (aim 400–1,000), first line ≤125 chars, ≤5
hashtags, "link in bio". Formats: `carousel` (6–10 slides), `image` (1 slide), `reel` (script required, 20–45s;
cover slide(s) with size `story`), `story` (3–5 frames, size `story`). Save-worthy, visual, punchier than LinkedIn.
Hashtag pools — LinkedIn: #PerformanceMarketing #D2C #D2CIndia #CreativeStrategy #MetaAds #AdCreative
#MarketingAnalytics #AIinMarketing #BrandMemory #CreatorMarketing #GrowthMarketing.
Instagram: #performancemarketing #d2cindia #metaads #adcreative #marketingtips #creativestrategy #ugcads
#digitalmarketingindia #adstrategy. Brand: #KnowWhyYourAdsWin.

## 6. Creative (images) — template is fixed; you only supply slide content
Every image is rendered from one template: violet gradient, gold accents, **Galvor logo pill top-left**, footer with
author chip (LinkedIn personal posts) or #KnowWhyYourAdsWin, and galvor.ai / "Swipe →". Wrap words in `*asterisks*`
to highlight them in gold (balanced pairs only, 1–2 highlights per slide). Sizes: `feed` 1080×1350 (default),
`story` 1080×1920 (reel covers, stories, LinkedIn video covers).

Layouts and fields (headline max chars in brackets):
- `cover` [95]: kicker, headline, sub, play (story size only: button text e.g. "Watch the breakdown")
- `statement` [110]: headline (big quote), sub
- `text` [80]: kicker, headline, body (use "\n\n" between short paragraphs; ≤60 words)
- `stat` [90]: kicker, stat (e.g. "36%"), headline (what the number means), sub, **source (required)**
- `list` [70]: kicker, headline, items (2–6; "Title — detail" strings, ≤70 chars each)
- `compare` [60]: kicker, headline, left_title, left (2–4 short lines), right_title, right (2–4 short lines)
- `poll` [90]: kicker, headline, options (2–4), sub
- `festival` [70]: motif (one emoji e.g. 🪔 ✨ 🎄 🪁 🇮🇳 🌾 🎆), kicker, greeting (≤22 chars, e.g. "Happy Pongal"),
  headline (short line tying festival to Galvor's idea), sub
- `cta` [80]: headline, sub, actions (1–3 short strings). **The first action is always "Follow Galvor"**
  (LinkedIn) or **"Follow @galvor.ai"** (Instagram); the others can be save/comment/DM/link asks.
Carousel recipe: slide 1 `cover` (hook) → 4–7 value slides (list/stat/compare/text/statement) → last slide `cta`.

## 8. Decoded by Galvor — the newsletter (weekly, every Wednesday 08:30 IST)
Posted as a LinkedIn newsletter from the **Galvor page** and sent by email. Author line: "By Raj Dhakad, Founding AI
Engineer, Galvor AI · Decoded by Galvor, Issue #N". The format is fixed — Issue #4 (11 Nov 2026) is the reference and the
Studio's Decoded editor reproduces it exactly (tools/test_decoded.py). Every issue, in order:
1. Masthead "Decoded / by Galvor AI · Issue #N / Missed Issue #N-1? <link>", double rule.
2. H1 "Decoded #N: <Title>" — contrarian and specific ("Fake Followers: 0%. Why Your Creator Still Won't Sell").
3. Italic standfirst (one paragraph), byline, boxed **Key takeaways** (exactly 3).
4. Cold open "Monday, H:MM a.m." with **Sam**, the recurring growth lead: 3 short paragraphs + one italic turn line.
5. 3–5 sections: what the industry says (every outside number linked to its source in the same sentence) → what Galvor saw
   in its own data (charts with full-sentence alt text + "Source:" caption) → how Galvor approaches it.
6. "The shift for marketers and leaders": "Five moves Galvor recommends…" + five bold-lead bullets.
7. Double rule, "Next issue: <title>" + two-sentence teaser (and the next issue must deliver it).
8. Dashed CTA box with a free offer (audit / shortlist) + black button to talk.galvor.ai.
9. "How we did this" (method, dataset, dates, definitions) and "Sources" (Publisher (date), linked title), then
   "Previously in Decoded".
Length 1,900–2,500 words. No invented numbers: outside numbers only from linked sources, Galvor numbers only from §4 or a
saved analysis; otherwise [DATA NEEDED]. No client names.

## 9. Awareness playbook (the main goal: more views, impressions, comments and clicks on every handle)
- **Personal profiles carry the reach.** Aug–Oct 2026 data: Sunil's posts reached a median ~2,000 people vs ~345 for the page (his export lists his top posts, so the real gap is smaller, but it is large). The
  page's job is to reshare the week's best personal post (Fri 12:30) with its own line, not to compete.
- **First hour matters.** When anything goes live the Studio pings the rest of the team: one real comment (an
  opinion, a question, an example — never just "Great post") or a repost with a line of your own, within 60 minutes.
- **Reply to every comment** within the first hour (the Studio reminds the poster 30 min after it goes live).
- **One idea, many places.** Each Decoded issue gets a promo pack: LinkedIn share text, Raj's repost, team comment
  starters, an Instagram story on the day and a takeaways carousel. Strong LinkedIn posts get repurposed to Instagram.
- **Hooks:** first line ≤210 chars on LinkedIn, ≤125 on Instagram; open with a tension or a number from the facts bank.
- **Clicks:** links go in the first comment (LinkedIn) or bio/story link sticker (Instagram), always a tracked UTM link.
- **Measure weekly** on Results → Awareness: views, comments, clicks, reposts/saves, follows, against the weekly targets.
