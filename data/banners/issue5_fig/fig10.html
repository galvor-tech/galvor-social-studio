Describe the situation. Don’t diagnose the viewer.

Meta’s own example of where the line sits.
Allowed
✓“New diabetes treatment available”
Names the product and the need. The viewer decides whether it applies.
Not allowed
✕“Do you have diabetes?”
Implies the advertiser knows something personal about the viewer.
Meta’s rule: “ads must not contain content that asserts or implies personal attributes.”
Source: Meta Transparency Center, Privacy violations and personal attributes. Examples and rule quoted from the policy; the explanations under each are ours.
