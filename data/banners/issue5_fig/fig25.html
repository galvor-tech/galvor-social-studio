Four things to look for: the same reason, or a new one?

An ad gives a buyer a new reason when one of these four changes. A new backdrop or a new story doesn’t count.
The wantThe result in their life
Still the same reason
“Better sleep”, reworded as “deeper rest”
A new reason
Stay sharp at work. Be patient with my kids tomorrow.
The momentWhen the problem is real
Still the same reason
The same bedtime scene, reshot in a new room
A new reason
Awake at 3 a.m. The night before an early shift.
The doubtWhat nearly stops them
Still the same reason
No doubt answered, or the same one again
A new reason
“Will I be groggy?” “I have tried others.”
The proofWhy they believe you
Still the same reason
The same claim, said again
A new reason
A demo, a number, a customer’s own words
Illustrative examples for a sleep-supplement brand.
