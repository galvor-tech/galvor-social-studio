The one finding that holds up

A message matched to what the audience wants persuades more. The effect is real and modest.
702
experimental studies in one review
206,482
people across those studies
r = .20
average gain in persuasion from matching the motivation
Where it is strongest
The review found matching effects were “largest when matching to contextual factors”: the situation, more than a fixed trait.
Where it stops
These are experiments on attitudes, intentions and behaviour. None is a test of Meta ads for a D2C brand.
Source: Joyal-Desmarais et al., Psychological Bulletin, 2022.
