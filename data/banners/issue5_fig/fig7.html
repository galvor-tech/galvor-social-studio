Three things marketers repeat, checked

Each claim next to what its source says.
The claim
“95% of purchase decisions are subconscious.”
What the source says
The Harvard interview behind the figure says “95 percent of all cognition”, not purchases. A 2025 Research World article: “there is no evidence to back up this claim.”
HBS Working Knowledge, 2003; Research World, 2025
The claim
“People buy on emotion and justify with logic.”
What the source says
Across more than 2,000 TV ads for 144 car models, both informational and emotional content raised sales. Informational content did more for low-price cars.
Guitart and Stremersch, Journal of Marketing Research, 2020
The claim
“Sell to the reptile brain.”
What the source says
The idea of newer brain layers added over an old one “has long been discredited among neurobiologists”.
Cesario, Johnson and Eisthen, Current Directions in Psychological Science, 2020
