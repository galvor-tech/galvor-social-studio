Distribution & engagement playbook

  • Target list: 100 named people per voice (Sunil → founders/CMOs, Raj → performance/growth leads, Shrutik → brand, creative and agency people).
  • Connections: 10–15 a day per person with a one-line personal note. No pitch.
  • First hour: the other three team members leave real comments; the author replies to everything; links go in the first comment.
  • Comments before posts: 20 thoughtful comments a week on target-audience posts.
  • DMs: only after someone engaged twice. Lead with something useful (a teardown, the brief template).
  • Company page: reshares the best post every Friday and keeps an archive of proof.
  • Reuse: every Friday the best post of the week becomes a carousel, a reel or a newsletter section.
  • No engagement pods or bought followers.
