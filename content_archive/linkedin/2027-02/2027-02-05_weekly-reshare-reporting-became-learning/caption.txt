A report that ends without a decision is a receipt.
It proves the money was spent and says nothing about Monday.

Most weekly ad reports carry the same columns: spend, impressions, clicks, cost per order, return. All of them describe what happened.

One change turns the same report into something a team can act on. Every row must end in one of four verbs.

Scale. It is working and has room.
Cut. It has had a fair run and is not paying.
Refresh. The message sells and the look is tired.
Test. We do not know yet, and here is the ad that will tell us.

A row that cannot take a verb does not belong in the report. That alone shortens most decks.

Then add one line under the table: what we learned this fortnight that changes the next brief. If that line is blank two reports running, the team is reporting and has stopped learning.

Agencies and in-house teams can make this change without new data or new tools. The numbers stay the same and the last column is new.

From dashboards to decisions is a short trip once every row has a verb.
