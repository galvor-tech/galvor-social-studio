A premium linen brand moved $3,800 of its ad spend in the first month of working with us.
The budget stayed the same size. Where it went changed.

The results after that month: RoAS up 67%. Hold rate up 25%.

Reallocation sounds like a media buyer's call. Here it started on the creative side.

The loop decoded every running ad and priced each attribute against cost per order. Then each ad got a call: scale, cut or shift. The brand's team moved the $3,800 by those calls, away from ads carrying the expensive attributes and towards the ones that had earned their place.

Hold rate is the number we watch closest. It says people stayed with the ads longer, so the RoAS gain comes with a reason the next brief can repeat.

One brand, one category, one month. Read it as a strong signal, and copy the method before the number.
