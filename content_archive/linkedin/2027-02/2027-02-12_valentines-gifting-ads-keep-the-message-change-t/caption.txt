Valentine's week is when gifting brands launch the most new ads in the fewest days.
Our data says how you change them matters more than how many you make.

In Galvor's analysis of video ads launched May to September 2026:

A proven message in a new look got 47% more first-month spend.

A new message over a familiar first shot got 16% less.

For gifting week, that suggests a simple rule. Keep the line that already sells, the one about delivery before the 14th or the gift they'll actually use. Change the first shot, the cast, the setting.

Then watch the count. Six or more look-alikes launched within three days each got 48% less first-month spend. A rushed Valentine's batch often looks exactly like that.

This data covers May to September, so it says nothing specific about gifting season. Treat it as a pattern to test this week, and keep notes on what it teaches you for next February.
