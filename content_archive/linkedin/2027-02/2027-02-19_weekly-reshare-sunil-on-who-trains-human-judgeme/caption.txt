Agents are taking over the work junior marketers used to learn on.
Sunil asked the question that follows from that this week, and it stayed with us.

If an agent writes the first draft of the report, sorts the creatives and suggests the next test, where does the next head of growth learn judgement?

Our answer at Galvor is to make the agent show its thinking. Vira explains why it suggests a call, so the person reviewing it learns the reasoning along with the result. Every call a person overrules goes back into Brand Memory, so the team's judgement is recorded too.

That only works if someone senior reviews the calls and says why they disagree. Judgement gets trained in those reviews.

Sunil's post is below. Worth reading if you lead a team that is handing work to AI this year, and worth sending to whoever trains your juniors.
