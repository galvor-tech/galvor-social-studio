Two days before Valentine's Day is the worst time to find a creator conflict.
The gifting video is approved, and then someone spots the creator's post for a rival brand from last week.

Gifting season is crowded. Chocolate, jewellery, fragrance and skincare brands all brief creators in the same few weeks, and the same faces show up in many feeds.

So I check for conflicts at the shortlist, before anyone gets attached to a creator.

Scroll the last few weeks of posts for anyone in the same category.

Ask the creator, in writing, what else they're running this month.

Check the brand's own list of competitors and categories it won't sit next to.

If the brand needs exclusivity, put the window in the agreement with dates.

Asking a creator about other brands feels awkward. Pulling a live video two days before the big day feels much worse.

It's also why Galvor Scout reads creators against the brief. In 8,673 creator checks, 66% of the creators turned down missed a stated must-have.
