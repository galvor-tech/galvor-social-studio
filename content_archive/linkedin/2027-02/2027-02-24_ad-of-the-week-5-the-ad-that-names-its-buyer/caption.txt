Ad of the Week #5: an ad that names its buyer in the first line.

This week's pick is a 20-second video from a home-fragrance D2C brand. No celebrity, no big production. A phone camera, a small apartment and one line of on-screen text in the first second.

The line doesn't describe the product. It describes the person: someone working from home in a small flat who notices the room feels stale by 4 p.m.

Here's what I tagged, frame by frame:

Hook: a situation, not a product. You know in one second whether this is about you.
Cast: one person, no performance. It reads like a colleague, not a model.
Pacing: the product appears by second 3, before the scroll decision is made.
Objection: "will it be too strong?" is answered out loud, halfway through.
CTA: one action. No offer stacked on top.

Why this matters now: Meta's ranking reads who an ad is for, not only how it looks. An ad that states its buyer, moment and reason to buy gives the system something to match.
