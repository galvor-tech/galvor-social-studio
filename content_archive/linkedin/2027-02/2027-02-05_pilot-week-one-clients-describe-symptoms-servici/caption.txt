In the first week of a pilot, clients describe symptoms.
My job is to find the step in their workflow that's causing them.

"Our creators are slow." "Our ads all look the same." "Approvals take forever."

Each of those is true. None of them tells me where to start.

So I ask to walk through one recent video, from brief to the day it went live. Who wrote the brief. Who picked the creator. How feedback travelled. Who said yes at the end. How long each step sat waiting.

Usually one step stands out.

Slow creators can trace back to a brief that arrived without a product sample. Ads that look the same can trace back to three teams briefing from one old reference. Slow approvals can trace back to a reviewer nobody had mapped.

Once you find the right step, the fix is often small. Finding it is the real work of a pilot's first fortnight.

That's why I'd rather follow one video end to end than read a stack of reports about all of them.
