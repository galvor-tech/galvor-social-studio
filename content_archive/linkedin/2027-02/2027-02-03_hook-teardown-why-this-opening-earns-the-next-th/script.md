# Hook teardown: the delete test

**Platform:** linkedin · **Format:** video · **Length:** 43s
**Voice:** Raj Dhakad

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a growth lead whose ads stop the scroll but lose people a few seconds later gets one test that shows why. On-screen title (first frame): 'Good hook. Why do they leave?' Expectation the title sets: one clear reason and something to try today. The script gives the reason in the first ten seconds, then beats it with a test that takes a minute and a fix for the hook itself. The example ad is made up (a protein bar) and drawn as a simple storyboard of panels. No real brand, no real ad, no results.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / dialogue | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Title text over a six-panel storyboard of a made-up protein bar ad. The first panel glows, the others are dim. | Your hook stops people. Then they leave at second five. Here's why. | Good hook. Why do they leave? |
| 4–8s | A thumb scrolling a phone feed stops on the first panel. | Most teams think a hook has one job. Stop the scroll. | The belief: stop the scroll |
| 8–13s | An arrow draws from panel one to panel two. Panel two lights up. | It has two. Stop them, and set up the very next shot. | A hook has two jobs |
| 13–18s | A short tag list appears beside the storyboard: hook, pacing, cast, call to action. The word hook is circled. | We tag the hook on its own, on every ad we decode. So here's one test. | One test |
| 18–23s | Panel one is crossed out and slides away. The remaining five panels play in order. | Delete the first three seconds. Play the rest. Does the ad still make sense? | Delete the first three seconds |
| 23–28s | The five panels sit complete without the first. The removed panel greys out at the side. | If yes, your hook was decoration. Nothing after it needed it. | Still makes sense? Decoration. |
| 28–33s | The greyed panel is wiped clean and a question mark is drawn on it. | That only finds the gap. To close it, write the hook as a question. | Write the hook as a question |
| 33–38s | New panel one: a person at a desk, clock at four, text of the question. New panel two: the bar being unwrapped at the same desk. | 'Why am I hungry by four?' Then shot two answers it, with the product. | Shot two answers it |
| 38–43s | Panels one and two join with a line. Galvor logo end card. | A hook is a promise. The next shot pays it. Follow Galvor for more. | Follow Galvor |

**Audio:** No music, or a very low instrumental bed under the voice.

**Notes:** Native LinkedIn upload, vertical, under 60 seconds. Burn in subtitles. Use the cover slide as the thumbnail. Numbers used: none. The storyboard is a drawn, made-up ad, so no real brand appears. About 100 spoken words.
