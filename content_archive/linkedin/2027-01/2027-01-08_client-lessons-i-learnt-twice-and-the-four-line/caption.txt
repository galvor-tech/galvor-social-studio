Some client lessons I learnt twice last year.
The second time always cost more than the first.

The pattern was the same each time. A brand tells me something on a call. I fix it on that video. The note lives in my head or in one WhatsApp thread. A month later a new creator, or a new teammate, makes the same miss.

So this year I'm trying one habit. Every lesson gets written down the day I learn it, in one place the whole team can see.

Each entry has four lines.
What happened.
What the brand actually wanted.
Where it goes next: the onboarding note for new teammates, the sales handoff, or a request to our product team.
Who owns that change.

The third line matters most. A lesson in my notes gets learnt again by the next person. A lesson in the onboarding note reaches every new teammate on day one.

Galvor is built on one idea for ads: every ad should make the next one better. I'm trying to hold client servicing to the same idea.
