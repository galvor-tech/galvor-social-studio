Happy Pongal. If your festive ad runs in Tamil, who on your team checked the Tamil?
For a lot of brands, the honest answer is nobody who reads it.

Regional versions often get made at the very end. The English cut is approved, the subtitles get translated, and the file goes live because the date is close.

Here's what I now add to QC for every regional version.

A native reader watches the full cut with sound, on a phone.

Subtitles get checked for sync, line breaks and words that got cut off.

Product names and offers get checked against the English approval, word for word.

Supers get room to breathe. Some scripts need more space than English, and a squeezed line looks careless.

The greeting itself fits the region. Pongal, Sankranti, Lohri and Bihu fall in the same week, and they are not interchangeable.

Then plan the regional version into the timeline from the brief. If it's added on the last day, it gets the last day's attention.

Wishing everyone celebrating a sweet Pongal.
