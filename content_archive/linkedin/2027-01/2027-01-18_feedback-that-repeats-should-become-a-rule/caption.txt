The second time you give the same feedback, stop giving it.
Write it down as a rule and put it at the top of the next brief.

Every brand has notes that come back campaign after campaign. Show the logo early. Product in the hand, never on a table. Each time one returns as feedback, the team pays for a round the last campaign already paid for.

Here is the method for anyone who reviews creator work.

Keep one running list per brand and call it Rules.

When a note shows up a second time, move it from the feedback thread into that list. Use the reviewer's own words and add the date.

Paste the list at the top of every new brief. Check a first cut against it before anyone gives an opinion.

Once a quarter, ask the brand which rules no longer hold and delete them. A list that only grows stops getting read.

This is the small, manual version of what we call Brand Memory at Galvor. Plenty of data, no memory is how one note ends up written six times.

Every ad should make the next one better. So should every round of feedback.
