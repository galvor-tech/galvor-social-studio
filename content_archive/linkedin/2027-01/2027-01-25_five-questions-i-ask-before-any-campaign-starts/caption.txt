Before any campaign starts, I ask five questions.

If one of them has no answer, the brief is not ready.

1. Who exactly is this for?
2. What is the one thing they should do?
3. What did the last campaign teach us?
4. What must be said, and what must never be?
5. Who says yes, and by when?

The third one gets skipped most easily, and it is the one that makes this campaign better than the last.

With Republic Day sales closing and new financial year plans opening, this is a good week to run your next brief past all five. The slides have what a good answer looks like for each.
