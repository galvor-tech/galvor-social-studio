Edit or reshoot? I used to answer that with my gut.
Now I ask four questions in order, and the answer usually falls out.

A reshoot costs a creator's day, a new date and sometimes the launch. An edit that can't reach the real problem costs a round, and then the reshoot anyway.

The questions sort a problem by where it lives. Words, order and pacing can usually be fixed in the edit. Anything that has to be on camera usually can't.
