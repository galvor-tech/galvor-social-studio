Decoded #9 is out: budget season.

February and March are when D2C marketing teams make the case for next year's budget. Finance asks a fair question: what did last year's creative actually buy?

Most teams answer with media numbers. RoAS, CAC, cost by channel. Very few can answer for the creative itself: which ideas worked, what they were worth, and what the next round should fund.

This issue is about making that case.

Inside:
→ why creative is the line item finance understands least
→ how to show what each creative idea earned, not just each campaign
→ separating the cost of making ads from the cost of learning nothing
→ a one-page format to take into the budget meeting
→ the questions finance will ask, and how to answer them first
