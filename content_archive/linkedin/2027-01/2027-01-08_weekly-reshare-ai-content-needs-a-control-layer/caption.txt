AI made ads cheap to produce. It also made look-alikes free.
For a D2C team using AI to multiply its creative output, the hard job has moved from making to choosing.

In our analysis of new video ads launched from May to September 2026, new ads that looked like many ads already running got 36% less first-month spend.

A generator fed on what you already ran tends to hand back more of what you already ran. Volume goes up and the account sees the same idea again.

So put three gates between the generator and the ad account.

Brand gate. With the logo covered, would a customer still recognise this as you?

Difference gate. Place it next to your live ads. If a stranger cannot tell it apart from one of them, it does not go live.

Learning gate. Write one line on what this ad will tell you that the others cannot. An ad without that line waits.

A person holds each gate. The model can prepare the evidence for all three.

An ad that gets through has a reason to exist, however it was made.
