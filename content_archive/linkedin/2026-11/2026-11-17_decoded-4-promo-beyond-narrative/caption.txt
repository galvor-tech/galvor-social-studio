Decoded by Galvor, Issue #4: Beyond Narrative. Out today.

In Issue #3 we showed why Meta now wants new buyers, not new looks. Thirty ads that only change the backdrop still speak to one buyer with one reason, so the system treats them as close to one ad.

Issue #4 picks up from there.

If the story isn't the only thing that makes an ad new, what else is? This issue goes past the storyline and into the parts of an ad that teams rarely brief on purpose, and shows how to read them in your own account.

Written for founders, growth leads and creative teams who want fewer, better ads instead of more versions of the same one.
