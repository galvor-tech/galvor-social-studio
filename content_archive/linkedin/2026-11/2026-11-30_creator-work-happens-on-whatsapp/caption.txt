Creator work in India runs on WhatsApp. No tool will change that.
So the useful question for a brand team is how to make a chat behave like a workflow.

The usual advice is to move creators into your project tool. A creator working with several brands will not keep a separate login for each one. They will reply where they already are.

Four habits make the chat do most of a tracker's work.

1. One group per campaign. Put the brand and the go-live date in the group name.

2. The brief is one pinned message. Must-haves first, as a numbered list, so a creator can reply “point 3 is not clear”.

3. One ask per message. Three asks in one paragraph get one answer.

4. Voice notes are fine for talking. Every decision gets typed back in one line: “Confirming: shoot on Thursday, opening option 2.”

Then the step that happens outside the chat. At the end of each day, every decision and every date moves into one tracker the brand can open.

The chat is where the work gets done. The tracker is where the client reads it.

Meet creators where they already work, and keep the record where the brand can find it.
