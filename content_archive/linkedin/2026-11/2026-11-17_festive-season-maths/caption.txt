The most expensive creative mistake of the year happens in October and November.

Not because brands buy media badly. Because they move fast.

Festive season squeezes a quarter of learning into six weeks. Budgets go up. Timelines shrink. Teams ship more ads than in any other month.

And almost nobody stops to ask what those ads taught them.

The maths I keep coming back to:
More ads + less time to read them = the same lessons, paid for twice.

In January the dashboard says it was a good season. Revenue up, RoAS fine. Everyone moves on.

But the questions that matter for next Diwali never get answered:
Which hook carried the season?
Which offer brought buyers who came back?
Which ads only worked because demand was high anyway?

If you can't answer those by the end of November, next October starts from zero.

So this week, while the numbers are fresh, block one hour with your team. Not for a report. For three decisions: what we scale next year, what we never run again, and what we test before the next peak.

Every ad should make the next one better. Festive ads most of all.
