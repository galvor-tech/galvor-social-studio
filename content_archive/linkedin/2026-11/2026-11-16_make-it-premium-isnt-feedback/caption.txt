“Make it premium” isn't feedback until we define premium.

I've heard it a lot in my first weeks at Galvor. Different brands. Completely different meanings.

For one, premium meant slower: fewer cuts, more breathing room.
For another, it meant fewer words on screen.
For a third, it meant the creator should look less like a creator and more like a customer.

If we had acted on the word alone, we'd have shipped three wrong edits.

What I do now, before anyone opens an editing tool:

1. Ask for one ad they think is premium. Any brand, any category.
2. Ask what they'd remove from our draft to make it feel like that.
3. Write the answer down as a rule: “no more than six words on screen”, “hold the product shot for two seconds”.

That rule goes into the brief for the next round, and the one after that.

Feedback that lives only in a call gets repeated. Feedback written down as a rule gets used.

Which word do you hear in feedback that means something different every time?
