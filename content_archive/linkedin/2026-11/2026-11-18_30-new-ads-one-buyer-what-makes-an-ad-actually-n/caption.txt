30 new ads. One buyer. One reason. Meta counts that as close to one ad.

A growth lead asks the agency for thirty new ads by Friday. New backdrops, new colours, a static, a carousel, an unboxing. Two weeks later, the old ad still takes the budget.

Meta rebuilt the systems that pick ads this year. They now read who an ad is for, not only how it looks.

In the new video ads we analysed (launched May–Sep 2026):
→ Ads that looked like many running ads got 36% less first-month spend
→ A new message over a familiar first shot got 16% less
→ A proven message in a new look got 47% more

The middle one surprises people. Changing the script isn't enough if the first second looks the same.

A new ad needs a new buyer, a new moment or a new reason to buy, and a first shot that shows it.
