Decoded by Galvor, Issue #5: Creative fatigue. When to refresh, when to cut.

Every growth team has had the Monday where the best ad in the account suddenly costs more. The question is never whether it will happen. It's what you do next.

Refresh it and you might save a winner. Cut it and you might lose the ad that was carrying the account. Wait and the bill keeps growing.

Issue #5 is about making that call with evidence instead of instinct: the signals that show up before costs move, how to tell a tired ad from a bad week, and the smallest change that usually brings an ad back.
