Six weeks, a tidy deck, and nobody can say if it worked.
That is how most vendor pilots end, and the cause is usually visible on day one.

A pilot that starts without a decision attached cannot finish with one.

Before any pilot begins, with us or with anyone else, put five lines on one page:

The business question. One sentence a finance head would understand.
The baseline. The number today, and the period it comes from.
The yes result. What you will see if it works, and what you buy or expand when you do.
The no result. What you will see if it does not, and what you stop.
The end date, and the one person who makes the call.

The third and fourth lines carry the page. If both results lead to the same next step, the pilot answers nothing and you can skip it.

Year end is when pilots for the new quarter get agreed. Ask every vendor for this page before the kick-off call. A vendor who resists writing down the no result has told you something useful.
