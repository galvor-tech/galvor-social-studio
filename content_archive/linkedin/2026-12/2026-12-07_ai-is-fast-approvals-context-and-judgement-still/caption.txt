AI can make a first cut in minutes.
The approval still needs the same people and the same days.

For a brand team wondering what AI changes in creative work, this is the part to plan for: the making got shorter, so the waiting is now the longest stage.

When a video took a week to make, two days in someone's inbox went unnoticed. When it takes a morning, those two days are most of the timeline.

Three things are worth agreeing before anyone generates anything.

One named person per approval stage. A team inbox does not say yes. A person does.

What each person is reading for. Legal reads the claim. The brand lead reads the tone. Nobody reopens what someone else has cleared.

A reply-by time, and what happens when it passes.

Some of the work stays with people for a plain reason. The context that decides an approval is often written nowhere a model can read it: a claim that was pulled last quarter, a word the founder never wants on screen. A person has to carry that into the review.

A fast draft only helps if the yes can keep up with it.
