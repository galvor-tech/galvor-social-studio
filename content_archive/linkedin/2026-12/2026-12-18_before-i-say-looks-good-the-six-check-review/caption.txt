"Looks good" is the most expensive thing I can say too early.
Once I say it, the video goes to the client, and every miss after that is mine.

So before I say it, I run the same six checks on every cut.

Claims go first, because those are the misses that come back from legal. Then the product, the CTA, the words on screen, the sound, and finally the brief itself.

It felt slow the first few times. After a few weeks it became habit, and it's the same list I'd want any agent to run before it reaches me.
