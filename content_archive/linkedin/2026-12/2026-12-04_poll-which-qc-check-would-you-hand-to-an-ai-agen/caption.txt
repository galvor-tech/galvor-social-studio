Which QC check would you hand to an AI agent first?
We're working this out at Galvor right now, and I'd like to know where other teams draw the line.

Before a video goes to a client, someone checks it. Subtitles against the audio. The logo and the product in frame. Every claim against what the brand approved. The CTA on the end card.

Some of that is pattern work. A misspelt word, a missing logo, a frame where the pack faces the wrong way. A machine can stay patient with that in a way I can't at 11 pm.

Some of it needs judgement. Does this line sound like the brand? Will this claim make the legal team nervous?

My working rule so far: the agent flags, a person decides, and that person's name goes on the approval.

Vote below. If you picked the last option, tell me why in the comments. I'd like to hear the case for keeping it all human.
