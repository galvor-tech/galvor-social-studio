A quarterly creative review looks at ads that have already been replaced.
A weekly one looks at ads that have not settled. We run ours every two weeks.

Two weeks is long enough for a new ad to gather data you can read, and short enough for the finding to change the next brief.

Four parts, same order every time:

Decode. What ran, and what each ad was made of: hook, cast, pacing, setting, CTA.
Prove. Which attributes moved cost per order, and which only looked good.
Create. The next briefs, written from what was proved.
Run. What to scale, what to cut, where to shift spend.

One rule: the meeting ends when the next brief has changed because of it.

You can run this from a spreadsheet. The cadence is what most teams are missing.
