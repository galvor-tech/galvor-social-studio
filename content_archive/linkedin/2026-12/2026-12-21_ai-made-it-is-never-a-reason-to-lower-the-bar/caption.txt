“AI made it” is never a reason to lower the bar.
The person scrolling past your ad has no idea how it was made, and would not care if they did.

The myth is that an AI video deserves a lighter check because it was quick and cheap. The fact runs the other way. It needs the same check as any creator video, and three more.

The same for every video, whoever made it:
Is the claim the approved claim, word for word?
Is the product shown the way it really looks?
Is the ask clear in the last seconds?

Extra for AI:
The pack. Letters on the label, the logo shape and the pack colour drift in generated footage. Pause on every frame where the product is close.
Hands, faces and on-screen text, at the moments they move.
The voice. Listen to how it says the brand name and any Indian word.

If a video fails one line, it goes back. No discount for the tool.

Here is what AI does change. A remake is quick. That is a reason to reject more often, since saying no now costs an hour and not a week.

The bar belongs to the brand, and the tool does not get a vote.
