Most people see the ad. Almost nobody sees the shoot day.

A reshoot is usually decided on the shoot itself, by what did not get filmed.

So the plan for the day carries four extra takes besides the main one:

1. Three openings for the same script, because the opening is the first thing to test.
2. The product up close, label readable, held still.
3. The approved claim, word for word, in one clean take. An edit cannot repair a paraphrased claim.
4. Ten seconds of the room with nobody talking, to cover cuts.

Tick all four before the creator packs up. After that, each missing one is a second shoot day.

Brief to Live is a series on what happens between the brief and the ad going live.
