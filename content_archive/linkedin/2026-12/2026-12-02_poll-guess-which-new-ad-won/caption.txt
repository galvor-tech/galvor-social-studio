Three kinds of new ad. Only one got more spend.
Your next brief probably contains all three.

We took every new video ad launched from May to September 2026 across the brands we work with, and looked at how much spend each one got in its first month.

Then we sorted them into three kinds.

A. A proven message, shot in a new look.
B. A new message, over a familiar first shot.
C. A new ad that looks like many ads already running.

One kind got clearly more first-month spend. The other two got less.

It matters this week because sale season has just ended, and December briefs tend to be written from the looks that are already in the account.
