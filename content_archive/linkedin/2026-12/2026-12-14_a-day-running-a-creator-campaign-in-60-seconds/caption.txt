A creator campaign day is four check-ins around the shoot.

Morning: the brief goes out with the must-haves at the top.

Midday: questions come back. If two creators ask the same thing, the brief has a hole, so the brief gets fixed and then the replies go out.

Afternoon: first cuts. Claim, product and ask get checked before the brand sees anything.

Evening: a follow-up by voice note, and every decision from the chat goes into one tracker.

The one to copy tomorrow is the midday rule. The same question twice is a note about your brief.

This is Brief to Live, a series on what happens between the brief and the ad going live.
