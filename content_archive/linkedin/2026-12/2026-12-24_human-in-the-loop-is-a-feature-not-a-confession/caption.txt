Our AI co-worker can recommend a budget move. It cannot make one.
That was a design choice, and I would make it again.

The fair doubt about any agent in a marketing team is simple: what happens when it is wrong?

My answer is to decide, before building anything, where a person stands. Three questions sort it.

Can the mistake be undone for free? A wrong tag on an ad can. Let the system act, and review in bulk.

Does the mistake spend money? A budget shift does. The system shows its reasoning and a person approves. Galvor never touches the media budget.

Could the mistake hurt the brand? A claim in a script could. A person reads it before it goes anywhere.

The engineering work is making that review quick: the recommendation, the evidence and the expected result in one place. Someone who can see why can say yes or no in a couple of minutes.

If you are evaluating an agent this quarter, ask the vendor to draw this map. Where does it act alone, and where does it wait?

An agent you can overrule is one you can let near real money.
