The most useful hour in my week this year was usually an onboarding call.
On the last day of 2026, it's the habit I'm keeping.

A sales call tells you what a client hopes the product will do.
Onboarding shows you what they actually do with it on day one.

Which screen they open first. Which word they ask us to explain. The moment they stop and say, "So what do I do with this?"

That last question has shaped Galvor more than most roadmap meetings.

So my advice to any founder building for marketing teams: sit in on onboarding for as long as you possibly can. Say very little. Listen, and take notes your product team will actually read.

It's slow, and it won't scale forever. It is also where you find out whether you built what you think you built.

Thank you to every team that let us in this year, and trusted us with their ads and their questions.

See you in 2027.
