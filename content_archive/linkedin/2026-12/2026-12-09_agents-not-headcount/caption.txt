Most marketing headcount requests are requests for more hands.

Hands to move information from one place to another, which is the work software agents now do well.

Before I would approve another hire on a D2C marketing team, I would ask for one page: last week's hours, split into two columns.

Column one is moving information. Pulling reports, tagging creatives, rebuilding the review deck, chasing files and approvals.

Column two is deciding. What to make next, what to scale, what to stop, which risk is worth taking this quarter.

I expect the first column to be the longer one on most teams. That column is where agents belong. They do not tire on the fortieth creative.

The second column is where people belong, and it rarely gets a full day of anyone's week.

So the planning question for 2027 changes. Start with the decisions you want made better. Then ask whether each one needs a person or an agent.

Hire for the second column. Automate the first.
