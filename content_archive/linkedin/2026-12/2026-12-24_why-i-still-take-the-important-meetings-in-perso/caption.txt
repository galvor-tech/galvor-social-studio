Some of our best product decisions this year started on a local train to someone's office.
In an AI-first company, I still take the important meetings in person.

A video call gets you answers to the questions you asked.
A room gets you the things nobody planned to say.

The pause when a founder mentions last quarter's budget.
The creative manager who pulls up the ad she is proudest of before you ask.
The question at the door, on the way out, that turns out to be the real one.

None of that makes it into a transcript. Agents can summarise a meeting very well. They can't sit in one.

It's a quiet week, so a lighter thought for anyone planning January.

There is probably one meeting you keep moving to a call. A client, a partner, someone on your own team. That's likely the one to have in person.

Thank you to everyone who made time for us in 2026, in rooms and on screens. Happy holidays.
