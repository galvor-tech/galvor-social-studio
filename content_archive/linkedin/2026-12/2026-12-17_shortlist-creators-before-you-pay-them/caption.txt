A creator can have real followers and still sell nothing for you.
Most shortlists check the audience. Few check the videos.

Before you agree a fee, watch the creator's last ten videos and ask:

1. Do the first three seconds give a reason to keep watching, or only a greeting?
2. Have they sold in your category or price range before?
3. On paid posts, do the comments ask about the product?
4. Do they speak your buyer's language and know their context?
5. Is there a format they repeat well, for your second video to build on?

A creator who passes four of the five is worth a paid test video. One who passes two is a guess with an invoice attached.

The audit costs you an evening. A wrong creator costs the fee and the month.
