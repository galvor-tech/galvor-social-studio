Ad of the Week #7: a great creator doing the wrong job.

This week's ad is from a home water-purifier D2C brand. The creator is excellent. High energy, quick cuts, great on camera. The video has every sign of a strong post: a fast hook, trending audio, a big reaction shot.

As an ad, I think it's asking that energy to do a job it isn't built for.

A water purifier is a considered purchase. The buyer is comparing models and worried about installation, maintenance and whether the claims are true. They aren't looking to be excited. They're looking to be reassured.

What I tagged:
Hook: a reaction shot. It grabs attention but doesn't say who the ad is for.
Proof: the claim is spoken fast, over music. No demonstration.
Objection: installation and maintenance never come up.
Pacing: built for a scroll, not for a decision.

The same creator could make a strong ad for this brand, with a different job: a calm walk-through of installation, in their own voice, at their own home.

Creator fit isn't about the creator. It's the match between their style and the doubt the buyer has.
