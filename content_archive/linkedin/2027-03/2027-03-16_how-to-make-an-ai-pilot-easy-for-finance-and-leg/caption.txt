How to make an AI pilot easy for finance and legal to say yes to.

Most AI pilots in marketing don't die in the demo. They die three weeks later, in an email thread between finance, legal and the marketing head.

I've been on both sides of that thread. The questions are almost always the same, and almost never answered in the proposal.

What data will you access, and what won't you touch?
What does it cost, and when can we stop?
Who signs off on anything the system recommends?
What does success look like on day 30, in one number?
What happens to our data if we end the pilot?

When the proposal answers these before they're asked, the pilot moves in days, not weeks.

What we've learned to put on page one:
→ one business question the pilot will answer
→ a fixed end date and a fixed fee
→ read-only access wherever possible
→ a human approves every recommendation
→ a written exit: what's returned, what's deleted

None of this is clever. It's respect for the people whose job is to say no.
