An escalation that went better because we raised it early.

Midway through a creator campaign, one creator delivered a video that didn't match the brief. Good energy, wrong message. The product claim was off, and that creator's shoot window was closing.

I had two options. Fix it quietly with a reshoot request and hope it landed on time. Or tell the brand that day, before they saw the video.

I told them that day. Three lines: what happened, what it changed, what I proposed.

What I expected: a difficult call.
What happened: the brand manager suggested a simpler fix than mine. Use the strongest seconds as a cut-down for a different placement, and brief a second creator for the main message.

We launched on the original date.

The lesson I took: an escalation isn't a confession. It's an invitation to solve the problem with more people who care about it. Raised early, it gets better ideas. Raised late, it gets blame.

I now flag anything that changes a date, a deliverable or a cost on the day I find out.
