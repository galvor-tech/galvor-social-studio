Ad of the Week #8: one ad, tagged end to end, and the one word it ends in.

Every teardown I do should end in a decision. Not "interesting ad". One of four words: scale, cut, refresh or test.

This week's ad is a 25-second video for a beard oil from a men's grooming D2C brand. It has been running for a while, in a few versions.

Here's how I tag it:

Hook: a close-up of a patchy beard and one line: "three weeks in, still patchy?" Clear buyer, clear doubt.
Cast: one man in his twenties at a bathroom mirror. Feels like the buyer.
Proof: a before-and-after at second 12. Strong, but small on a phone screen.
Pacing: the product is named at second 8. Late for a buyer who's already sceptical.
CTA: an offer and a quiz at once. Two asks.

My word: refresh.

The hook and cast are doing the work. Keep them. Bring the product forward, make the before-and-after full-screen, and give it one ask.

How long an ad has run doesn't tell me it's winning. The attributes tell me which parts are worth keeping.
