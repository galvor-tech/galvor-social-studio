Sunil's post this week is a good one to save before your next competitor review: your competitors' ads are a free research report, if you read them honestly.

Three ways to read them better, from his post.

See what's visible, and only that. You can see what's running, how often it changes and what it says. You can't see budget, results or why it was made.

Look for movement, not snapshots. What did a competitor stop doing? What did they test three versions of? Iteration shows where they're paying attention.

Read for the buyer. Who is each ad for, and what reason to buy does it give? Gaps show up as buyers nobody in your category is talking to.

We never claim a competitor's spend or results from public data. A careful read tells you which questions to test on your own ads.
