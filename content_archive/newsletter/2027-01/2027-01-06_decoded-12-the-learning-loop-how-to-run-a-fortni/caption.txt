Learn, create, measure, learn.

Most teams do the middle two. They make ads and they read dashboards. The learning on either side, deciding before you make and writing down what you found after you measure, is the part that quietly gets skipped.

In Issue #8 of Decoded, Raj walks through a fortnightly creative cycle day by day: decode, decide, brief, produce, run, review. Plus the 30-minute review meeting that ends every cycle in four words: scale, cut, refresh, test.
