The brief said "test a new opening". The feedback said "can the logo be bigger?"

It happens on every team, ours included. By the time the video comes back, three weeks have passed and nobody has the brief open.

So the ad gets judged on taste, and the thing it was made to test gets edited out.

A small fix: paste the brief's goal as the first line of the feedback doc. Then sort every note into two piles. Notes that help the test. Notes that are just preference.

Do the first pile. Park the second.
