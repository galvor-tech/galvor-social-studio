Be honest. How does your team decide which ad to cut?

1. Gut feel
2. Worst number last week
3. A rule we wrote down
4. Whoever spoke last in the meeting

No wrong answers. Many teams are a mix.

One reason to move towards 3: in our analysis, some ad styles started about 35% dearer per order in their first two months and ended close to average over their life. Cut on week one and you never see that.

So write the rule before launch: how long, and against which number.
