30 new ads. One buyer. One reason. Meta counts that as close to one ad.

Meta rebuilt how it picks ads this year. It now reads who an ad is for, not only how it looks.

So a new backdrop, a new colour and a fresh carousel don't make an ad new. A new buyer, a new moment or a new reason to buy does.

In the ads we analysed from May to September, new ads that looked like many running ads got 36% less first-month spend. A proven message in a new look got 47% more.
