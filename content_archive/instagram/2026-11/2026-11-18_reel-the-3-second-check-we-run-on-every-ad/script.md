# Reel: the 3-second check we run on every ad

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 32s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Phone on a tripod at eye level, window light, lapel mic. Screen recordings of a public ad from the Meta Ad Library with the brand name and logo blurred (or one of our own demo ads). Edit in CapCut with Galvor violet captions.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / dialogue | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Close-up talking head. A feed scrolls fast on a phone held up to camera. | You get about one swipe to earn the next second. Here's what we check first. | The 3-second check |
| 3–9s | Screen recording: ad paused on frame one; a gold circle draws around the face. | One. Is there a face in the first frame? People stop for people. | 1. Face in frame one |
| 9–15s | Same ad with the mute icon highlighted; subtitles pop in. | Two. Can you follow it on mute? Plenty of people scroll with the sound off. | 2. Works on mute |
| 15–22s | Timeline scrubber stops at 0:03; product slides into frame. | Three. Is the product or the problem on screen by second three? Or is your hook selling someone else's story? | 3. Product by second 3 |
| 22–28s | Back to talking head, slight push-in. | Miss one and the ad isn't broken. Fix it before you put more money behind it. | Fix it before you scale it |
| 28–32s | End card: Galvor logo pill top-left, handle centred. | Follow Galvor for a decode every week. | Follow @galvor.ai |

**Audio:** Low-key lo-fi or trending instrumental at 10–15% under the voice.

**Notes:** Keep cuts every 2–3 seconds. Burn in captions. Blur any brand names in screen recordings.
