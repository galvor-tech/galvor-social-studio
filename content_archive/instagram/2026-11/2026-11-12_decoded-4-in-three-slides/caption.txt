Fake Followers: 0%. Why Your Creator Still Won’t Sell

1. Finding creators is solved. Meta alone now lists more than 10 million, and most tools turn a brief into a list. Picking the ones who fit your brand, are trusted and can sell is not.

2. Clean numbers don’t predict fit. Of the creators Galvor checked whose engagement was in the top band for their size, 62% still weren’t right for the brief.

3. The work after the shortlist (scripts, reviews, revisions, tracking) still runs on spreadsheets, email and DMs for almost half of brands.
