# Reel: three rules our team works by

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 40s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a founder or growth lead curious how a creative intelligence team thinks gets three working rules to borrow for next year's planning. On-screen title: Three rules we work by. Expectation set: a culture video. The script beats it because each rule changes a decision about ads. Outline: small sample is a signal (second-best, first); price the attribute, not the ad (best, second); every ad should make the next one better (the close).
