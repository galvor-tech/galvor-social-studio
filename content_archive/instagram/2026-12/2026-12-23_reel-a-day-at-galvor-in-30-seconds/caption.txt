Three rules our team works by. All three are about ads.

1. A small sample is a signal. One good week earns a second test.
2. We price the attribute, not the ad. The hook, the cast and the pacing are each checked against cost per order.
3. Every ad should make the next one better. An ad that taught nothing cost more than its spend.
