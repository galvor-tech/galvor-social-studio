Before you add AI to a creative team, write down which decisions stay with people.

Most teams are comfortable with AI doing the reading: tagging ads, counting what repeats, flagging what is slowing down.

The doubt starts when a tool changes spend or approves creative and cannot say why.

So agree the split first. AI reads and suggests, with reasons. A named person decides anything that spends or goes live.

That is how we built Galvor: it never touches the media budget.
