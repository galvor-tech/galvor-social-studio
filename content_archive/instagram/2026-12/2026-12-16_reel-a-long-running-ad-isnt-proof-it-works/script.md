# Reel: old competitor ad? Two checks before you copy it

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 42s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a performance marketer about to copy a competitor's long-running ad learns why the run length proves little and what to check instead. On-screen title: Old competitor ad? Don't copy yet. Expectation set: a reason to pause and something better to do. The script gives the reason in the first two scenes, then beats the expectation with two checks and a way to test. Outline: live does not mean working; remakes are the stronger hint (second-best, first); the pattern across brands; your own small test (best).
