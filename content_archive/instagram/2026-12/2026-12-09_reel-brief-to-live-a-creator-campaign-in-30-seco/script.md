# Reel: the four-line creator brief

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 38s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a brand or creative manager whose creator videos come back sounding like the brief read aloud gets a brief short enough to act on. On-screen title: The four-line creator brief. Expectation set: four lines I can copy. The script gives all four, then beats the expectation with the fifth point: everything not in the brief belongs to the creator. Outline: buyer and reason to buy (what to say); first shot (how it opens); the one no-go; the rest is theirs.
