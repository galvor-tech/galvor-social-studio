The year-end deck: 40 slides on what happened. One missing slide on why.

We have all built this deck. The charts are right. The numbers tie. And the first question in the room is still "so what do we do in January?"

One slide answers it. Three headings:

What we would repeat.
What we would stop.
What we still do not know.

Put it second, right after the title. The third heading is the brave one, and it usually gets the best discussion.
