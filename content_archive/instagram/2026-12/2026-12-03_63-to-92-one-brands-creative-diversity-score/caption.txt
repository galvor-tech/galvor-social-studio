One brand's creative diversity score went from 63 to 92 out of 100.

That was two months after it made the changes Galvor recommended. It is one brand, so treat it as a signal and not a promise.

What the score is asking: are your ads different from each other, or the same ad in new colours?

A quick check you can run today. Put the first frames of your live ads side by side. Count how many you could tell apart with the sound off. If the honest answer is two, you are running two ads.
