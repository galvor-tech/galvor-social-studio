# Reel: three questions your dashboard can't answer (Ask Galvor)

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 38s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a head of performance who has plenty of dashboards and suspects another tool means another chart gets three questions that change a brief, and sees that Ask Galvor takes questions in plain words. On-screen title: Three questions dashboards can't answer. Expectation set: three questions. The script delivers them and beats the expectation by making each one usable without any tool. Outline: what winners share (second-best, first); what we stopped testing; what we have never tried with this buyer (best); Ask Galvor.
