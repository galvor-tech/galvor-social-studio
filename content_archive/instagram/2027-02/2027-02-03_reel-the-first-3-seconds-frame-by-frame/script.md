# Reel: the first three seconds, one second at a time

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 40s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a video editor or creative strategist who puts the new idea in the middle of the ad learns what each of the first three seconds has to do. On-screen title: Your ad's first three seconds. Expectation set: a breakdown of an opening. The script beats it with a number from our analysis and a job for each second. Outline: familiar first shot costs spend (our finding); second one, a new picture; second two, the buyer or problem (second-best); second three, the reason to stay (best).
