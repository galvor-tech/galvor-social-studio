Yesterday was Data Privacy Day. Five questions to ask any AI tool before it reads your ad account.

Your ad account holds what your brand has paid to learn. Before you connect it, ask what the tool can read and change, whether your data trains anything other brands benefit from, where it is stored, which other providers it passes through, and what happens when you leave.

Ask for the answers in writing.
