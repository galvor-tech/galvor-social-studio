One resolution for 2027: write three lines every time you switch an ad off.

What we expected. What we saw. What we will do next time.

Dashboards record what an ad did. They do not record what your team concluded, so the same lesson gets paid for twice.

Keep one shared sheet with one row per ad, written by whoever switches it off. Read the last ten notes before every brief.

Every ad should make the next one better. Happy new year from all of us at Galvor.
