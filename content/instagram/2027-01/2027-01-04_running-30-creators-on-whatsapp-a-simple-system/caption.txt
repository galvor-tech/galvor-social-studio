Creator work on WhatsApp goes wrong in the same few places. Five rules fix them.

The brief gets buried, feedback comes from more than one person, and nobody is sure which file is final.

You do not need a new tool for this. Use one group per creator per campaign, one pinned brief, one person giving numbered feedback, versioned file names, and a one-tab tracker outside the chat.
