# Reel: AI made the ad. Who briefed it?

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 39s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a creative or growth lead who has started generating ads with AI and wonders whether more versions is the answer learns what to feed the tool so the output is worth running. On-screen title: AI made it. Who briefed it? Expectation set: an opinion on AI ads. The script beats it with three inputs to add before the next batch. Outline: speed repeats the brief (the turn); proven reason to buy (second-best, first); first shots to avoid; one question per batch (best).

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / dialogue | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | A generate button is pressed. Ad tiles pour out faster than they can be counted. | AI can now make ads faster than any team. | AI made it. Who briefed it? |
| 4–8s | The tiles stack into a tall pile. A small trophy icon sits on top with a question mark. | So the belief goes: more ads, more winners. | More ads, more winners? |
| 8–12s | A one-line brief feeds the machine. Every tile that comes out looks the same. | Speed only repeats the brief. A thin brief, many twins. | Thin brief, many twins |

| 17–22s | Slot one fills with a line of ad copy marked as proven. | One: the reason to buy that has already sold for you. | 1. Your proven reason |
| 22–27s | Slot two fills with a row of used opening frames, each marked with a cross. | Two: the first shots you've used most, so it avoids them. | 2. First shots to avoid |
| 27–31s | Slot three fills with a single question mark, larger than the others. | Three matters most: the one question this batch should answer. | 3. One question per batch |
| 31–35s | The machine runs again. The tiles come out visibly different, each tagged with the question. | Now every ad it makes can teach you something. | Every ad teaches something |
| 35–39s | End card with the Galvor logo. Chime. | Generate less, learn more. Follow @galvor.ai. | Follow @galvor.ai |

**Audio:** Light instrumental bed, low under the voice. Soft chime on the end card only.

**Notes:** AI voiceover over motion graphics. No presenter and no first person singular. Captions burned in for muted viewing. Use dummy ad tiles only, never a client account or name. No numbers used. Do not name any AI tool or vendor.
