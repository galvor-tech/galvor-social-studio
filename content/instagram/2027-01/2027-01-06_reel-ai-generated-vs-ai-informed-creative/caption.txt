AI made the ad. Who briefed it?

AI makes ads faster than any team. Speed only repeats the brief, so a thin brief comes back as many versions of one ad.

Before you generate, give it three things from your own account:
1. The reason to buy that has already sold for you.
2. The first shots you have used most, so it avoids them.
3. The one question this batch should answer.

Then whatever the result, the batch teaches you something. Follow @galvor.ai.
