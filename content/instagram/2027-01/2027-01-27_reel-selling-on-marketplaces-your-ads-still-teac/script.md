# Reel: marketplace seller? Two things your ads still tell you

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 41s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a D2C founder whose orders mostly come through marketplaces, and who has written off Meta ad data because the sale lands elsewhere, gets two things to pull from the ads and use on the listing. On-screen title: Marketplace seller? Read your ads anyway. Expectation set: a reason ads still matter. The script beats it with two places on the listing to use what the ads show. Outline: attention data is yours (the turn); first shot to listing image (second-best, first); reason to buy to title and first bullet (best).
