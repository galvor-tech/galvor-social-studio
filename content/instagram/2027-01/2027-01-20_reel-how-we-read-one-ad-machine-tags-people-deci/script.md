# Reel: how one ad gets read (machine tags, people decide)

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 42s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a brand or creative manager who worries that software will start judging the team's creative sees exactly where the machine stops and people take over. On-screen title: Machine tags. People decide. Expectation set: a look inside how an ad is read. The script meets it at once and beats it by answering the two fears buyers raise: who makes the creative call, and who controls the budget. Outline: the machine counts 50+ attributes; each is priced against cost per order; a tag can't choose the next ad (best); the budget stays with the brand.
