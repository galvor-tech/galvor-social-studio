Before the next brief goes out, ask one question: what will this ad teach us?

Most briefs say what to make. Very few say what the team wants to find out.

So add one line at the top of the brief:

"We are testing whether ___ works better than ___."

A new opening line against the old one. A founder on camera against a customer. One blank each, nothing more.

Now the ad has a job even if it loses. A losing ad that answers the question still makes the next one better.
