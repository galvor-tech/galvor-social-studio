# Reel: three questions for any AI ad advice (meet Vira)

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 40s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a head of growth who is being offered AI recommendations on which ads to scale or cut, and is ready to try one, gets three questions that separate advice from guesses, then meets Vira. On-screen title: Before you trust AI ad advice. Expectation set: a caution about AI. The script beats it with a usable test and a product that is built to pass it. Outline: what did you look at; what would you do (second-best, first); what would prove you wrong (best); Vira shows its thinking.
