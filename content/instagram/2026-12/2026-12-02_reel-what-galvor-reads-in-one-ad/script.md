# Reel: the sale is over, write three lines (Decoded #7)

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 39s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a D2C growth lead who has just closed a sale and is about to move on, so the next sale starts from a blank page. On-screen title: Sale over. Write three lines. Expectation set: three specific things to write down. The script meets it by the second scene and beats it by turning the three lines into the brief for the next sale. Outline: the reason to buy that carried the sale (second-best, first); the first shot that held (best, second); what got no spend.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / dialogue | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | A sale banner peels off a calendar. A single revenue figure, blurred, is filed into a folder. | The sale is over. Most teams save the revenue number. | Sale over. Write three lines. |
| 4–8s | The folder closes. A blank one-page note opens beside it with three empty lines. | That number can't brief your next sale. Three lines can. | Three lines brief the next sale |

**Notes:** AI voiceover over motion graphics. No presenter and no first person singular. Captions burned in for muted viewing. Use dummy ad tiles only, never a client account or name. Newsletter reel for Decoded #7 (The Sale Is Over. What Did Your Ads Learn?), out the same morning. No numbers used. Do not show real revenue figures or a real account.
