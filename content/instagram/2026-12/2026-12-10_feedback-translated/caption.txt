"Make it pop." We have all said it. Nobody can act on it.

Vague feedback is usually a real feeling in a hurry. The person is right that something is off. They just have not said where.

A simple format that saves a round of edits. Every note gets three parts:

Where: the second it happens.
What: the thing on screen.
Change: what you want instead.

"Make it pop" becomes "at 0:02, show the product before the logo." Your editor and your creator can do that today.
