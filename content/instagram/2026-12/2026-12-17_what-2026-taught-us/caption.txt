What a year of reading ads taught us, in four lines.

We studied the new video ads launched by brands we work with. Four patterns kept showing up:

A proven message in a new look did better than a new message over a familiar first shot.
New ads that looked like the ones already running got less spend.
Launching a batch of look-alikes together made it worse.
Some styles start expensive and settle close to average, so judge them slowly.

Thank you to every brand, creator and reader who learned with us this year.
