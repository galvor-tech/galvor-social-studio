Instagram stopped reporting reach the old way, and a lot of creator benchmarks broke quietly.

A creator who used to show 300K reach doesn't show 300K viewers now. Views count every play, viewers are people. Neither is the old reach number.

So reset your benchmarks from posts made after the change, ask creators for viewers, and say so when you compare with last year.
