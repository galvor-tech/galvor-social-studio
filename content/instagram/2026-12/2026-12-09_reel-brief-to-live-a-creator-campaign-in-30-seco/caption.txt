A creator brief that fits on one phone screen. Four lines.

1. Who it is for. One buyer, in plain words.
2. The one reason to buy.
3. The first shot: what viewers see first.
4. The one thing never to say or show.

Ten talking points can't fit in thirty seconds, so the creator ends up reading the brief aloud. Give them four lines and leave the rest to them. That is what you hired them for.
