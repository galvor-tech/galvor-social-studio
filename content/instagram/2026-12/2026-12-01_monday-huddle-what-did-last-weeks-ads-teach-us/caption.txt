Most Monday ad reviews answer "how much?" and stop there.

Spend. RoAS. Top ad. Next week's launches. All useful, and all about last week.

Try adding two lines to the same agenda next Monday:

One thing last week's ads taught us.
One thing we will do differently because of it.

It takes five minutes. Write both lines in the same doc every week, and in three months you have something a dashboard cannot give you: a memory.
