Last week of December, marketing edition.

The out-of-office is on. The ads are not. And "let's pick this up in January" has been said with full confidence at least once.

If you do one calm job this week, make it this: rename your ads.

Pick one pattern and use it everywhere. Product, message, format, creator, month. In that order, every time.

It is dull work. It is also the reason next year you can sort a whole account by message and see what carried it.
