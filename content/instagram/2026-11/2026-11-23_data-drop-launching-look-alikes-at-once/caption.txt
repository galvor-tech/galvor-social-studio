Six or more look-alike ads launched within three days: each got 48% less first-month spend.

That is from our analysis of new video ads launched May to Sep 2026 across brands we work with.

New ads that looked like many already running got 36% less. A proven message in a new look got 47% more.

Our reading: near-copies compete for the same people, and none gets a fair start.

Before your next batch, lay the first frames side by side. If two match, change one or hold it back.
