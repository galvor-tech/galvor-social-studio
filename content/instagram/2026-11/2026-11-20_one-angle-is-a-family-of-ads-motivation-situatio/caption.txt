“Better sleep” is one angle. At least three different people are buying it.

Most teams write one ad per angle. That ad speaks to one kind of buyer, and when it slows down the whole angle gets blamed.

Split the angle three ways before you brief it:
Motivation: why they want it.
Situation: when they need it.
Objection: what stops them.

Each answer is a different person and deserves a different ad, starting with a different first shot.
