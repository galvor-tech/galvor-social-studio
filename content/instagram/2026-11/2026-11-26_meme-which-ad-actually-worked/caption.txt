Sale week: every ad looks like a winner.

The week after is when you find out which ones were good and which ones were standing next to a big discount.

Before you crown anything from this week's numbers, do one small thing. Pick your top three sale ads and write down what each one had besides the offer: the first shot, the person, the message.

Then watch those three at full price. The ones that still hold are your real learning from the sale.
