# Reel: 30 new ads, Meta saw one (the first-frame test)

**Platform:** instagram · **Format:** reel · **Length:** 36s
**Voice:** Galvor

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a growth lead who ships dozens of new ads a month and watches spend stay on the old ones learns what counts as new, and gets a test to run before launch. On-screen title: 30 new ads. Meta saw one. Expectation set: I will find out why my new ads get no spend. The script meets it in the first line, then beats it with two numbers from our own analysis and a ten-second test. Outline (new points only): look-alikes got less spend (our finding); a proven message in a new look got more (second-best, first); the first-frame line-up test (best, second).
