30 new ads. Meta saw one.

We looked at every new video ad the brands we work with launched from May to September 2026. New ads that looked like ads already running got 36% less first-month spend. A proven message in a new look got 47% more.

The test to run before your next launch: line up the first frame of every ad with the sound off. If you can't tell two apart, that is one ad. Launch one.
