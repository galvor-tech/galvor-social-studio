"The ad worked." Lovely. Which part?

The first shot? The line the creator opened with? The offer? Payday?

Nobody is wrong for not knowing. A results sheet tells you which ad won. It does not tell you why.

One habit you can start on Monday: before the next brief, finish this sentence in writing. "It worked because ___."

If the blank stays empty, you have just found your next test.
