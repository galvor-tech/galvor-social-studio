Where a creator video loses its days between brief and live.

The shoot gets blamed. Late feedback is the costlier part, because a rewrite is quick and a reshoot is slow.

Three checkpoints that move feedback to the front:
1. Approve the script on paper before any camera rolls.
2. Name one person who gives the feedback, in one round.
3. Judge the first cut against the brief, never against taste.

Feedback becomes a yes or a no, and the video goes live. Follow @galvor.ai.
