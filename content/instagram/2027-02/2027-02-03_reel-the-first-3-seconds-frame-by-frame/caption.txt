The first three seconds of your ad, one second at a time.

Second 1: show something your running ads haven't shown.
Second 2: put the buyer or their problem on screen.
Second 3: give the reason to stay. Say what they will get by the end, in words on screen.

Why the opening gets this much care: in our analysis of new video ads launched May to September 2026, a new message over a familiar first shot got 16% less first-month spend.
