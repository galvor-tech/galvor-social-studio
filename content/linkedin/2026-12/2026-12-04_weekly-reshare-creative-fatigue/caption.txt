By the time cost per order moves, the ad is already tired.
This week it matters more than usual: the ads that carried your sale have been shown to the same people for days.

Cost per order is the last number to react. If you wait for it, you pay for the delay.

The order we check, earliest sign first:

Hook rate. Fewer people stay past the first three seconds.
Hold rate. Those who stay leave sooner.
Frequency. The same people are seeing it again and again.
Cost per order. Only now does the bill change.

Then one decision: refresh or cut.

Refresh when the hook has faded but the people who do watch still buy. The message is working and the opening is worn out. Keep the message and shoot a new first three seconds.

Cut when hook rate is steady and orders have still dropped. People are watching and choosing not to buy. A new opening will not fix that.

Look at the early numbers on Monday, before the bill tells you.
