Ask a performance team where Monday goes.
Most of it goes into a report someone skims on Tuesday.

That is the part of the week agents are starting to take over. Pulling numbers, tagging every ad, lining up this fortnight against the last one.

The more interesting question for a founder is what the team does with the time it gets back.

From what I see, it goes one of three ways.

The time fills up with more creatives. Same thinking, more volume.

The time goes into meetings about the report the agent wrote.

Or the team spends it on judgement. Which ad to scale. Which to cut. What the next brief should test, and why.

Only the third one pays for itself, and it rarely happens on its own. Someone has to put the decisions on the calendar, the way the report used to be.

At Galvor we run that as a loop every two weeks: decode, prove, create, run. The agent does the reading. People make the calls and write down the reasons.

Faster reporting is worth it when it buys better decisions.
