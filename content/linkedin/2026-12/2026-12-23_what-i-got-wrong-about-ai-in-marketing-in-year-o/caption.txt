We built Galvor so it never touches your media budget.

This year many tools in our category went the other way and now launch and manage ads themselves. Here is why we have not.

The first reason is checking. When the same system recommends a move, makes the move and reports the result, nobody outside it can test the advice. We would rather give the reasoning to your team and let them argue with it.

The second is accountability. A founder answers for the marketing budget to a board and to a bank balance. That answer should not be 'the tool decided'.

So we stop at the recommendation: scale this, cut that, shift this much, and here is why. Your team or your agency moves the money.

This costs us something. A tool that acts looks faster in a demo than a tool that explains.

I accept that trade. Speed is easy to buy. An explanation your team can check is harder to find, and it is what makes the next decision better.
