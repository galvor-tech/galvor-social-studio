Hook rate says an opening worked. It cannot say why.
The first three seconds can, if you write them down the same way every time.

Ad of the Week #2 is a method, shown on a made-up example: a festive gifting ad for a tea brand. No real brand and no real numbers.

I log four things about the opening of an ad. What is in the first frame. What the first words promise. Who is on screen. When the product shows up.

Same four fields, same order, every ad. After twenty ads you can sort the sheet and see which kind of opening your good ads share.

A quiet week is a good time to log this year's ads.
