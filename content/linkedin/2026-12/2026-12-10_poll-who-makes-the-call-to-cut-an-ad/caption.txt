When an ad stops working, who on your team makes the call to cut it?
I'm asking because that answer is changing quickly.

Agents can now spot a tired ad before anyone opens the dashboard. So the hard part is moving from finding the problem to owning the decision.

The teams that grow without adding headcount tend to draw that line clearly. The agent flags the ad and explains why. A named person decides, and writes down the reason.

Where the line is blurry, two things go wrong.

Ads run a week too long because nobody felt it was their call.

Or slow starters get cut early because an alert looked scary. In Galvor's analysis of video ads launched May to September 2026, some ad styles started about 35% dearer per order in their first two months and ended close to average over their life.
