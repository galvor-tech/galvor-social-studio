When your media buyer leaves, your ad learnings usually leave too.
The one belief we are carrying into 2027: what a brand learns from its ads should belong to the brand.

Most D2C teams have plenty of data and no memory. The numbers sit in the ad account. The reasons sit in one person's head, an agency's deck or a WhatsApp thread.

A test for this quiet week: imagine a new hire starts on 4 January. Using only what is written down, could they brief your next ad?

For each ad that mattered, they would need four things:

What the ad was made of.
What happened when it ran.
What the team decided because of it.
Where the file is.

If that record exists, your 2026 spend bought knowledge as well as orders. If not, January starts from zero.
