A year of ads is research you have already paid for.

Most teams can quote last month's RoAS. Fewer can say why their strongest ad did well, or which ads they would never run again.

Here is a test for your next review. Ten questions, laptops closed, 30 minutes. Each person writes their answers alone, then the room compares.

Seven or more answers that everyone agrees on: your brand remembers. Fewer than that: the learning is sitting in the ad account and in one or two heads.

The ten questions are in the slides. The December review is a good time to run it, while the sale is still fresh.
