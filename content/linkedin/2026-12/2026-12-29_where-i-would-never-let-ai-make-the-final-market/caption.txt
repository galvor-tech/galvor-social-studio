We build AI for marketing teams.
Some calls I would keep away from it.

The way I sort them is two questions.

Can we undo this within a week? And if it was wrong, would the numbers tell us?

When both answers are yes, let the agent do the reading and propose the move. Which ad is tiring. Which opening shot to test next. A mistake there shows up in the next report and gets corrected.

When either answer is no, a person makes the final call. Four examples from a D2C brand's week:

A health or results claim in an ad. Once it is seen, it cannot be withdrawn.

The price and the offer. Buyers remember a discount long after the campaign ends.

The face of the brand. Which creator or founder story stands for you is a question of trust.

Anything tied to a festival or a sensitive moment.

In each of these, the cost of being wrong does not appear on a dashboard.

A useful hour in the last week of the year: list ten decisions your team made this quarter and sort them with those two questions.
