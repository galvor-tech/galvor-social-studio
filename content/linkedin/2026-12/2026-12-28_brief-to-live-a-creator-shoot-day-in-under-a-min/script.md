# Brief to Live: four extra takes that save a reshoot

**Platform:** linkedin · **Format:** video · **Length:** 45s
**Voice:** Shrutik Rathod

**Setup / shoot notes:** Idea: a brand or creative manager loses days to reshoots because the shoot captured only the main take. On-screen title (first frame): 'The shoot day nobody sees'. Expectation it sets: a look behind a creator shoot. The first line meets it; the video beats it with four extra takes to film so the edit rarely needs a reshoot. Order: two easy takes first, then a re-hook, then the claim take, which matters most. Shot vertically on a phone during a real creator shoot, with permission. Blur faces, product names and brand names unless cleared.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / dialogue | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | A creator setting up a phone on a tripod at home. Title text sits on the first frame. | Everybody sees the ad. Here is the shoot day behind it. | The shoot day nobody sees |
| 5–10s | A printed shot list on a table with four lines under the main take. | Most plan for one good take. Plan four extra ones and you rarely need a reshoot. | Four extra takes |
| 10–15s | The creator says a first line to camera three different ways, shown as three quick cuts. | One. Three openings for the same script. The opening is what gets tested first. | Three openings, one script |
| 15–20s | Close shot of a product held still in a hand, label facing the camera (blurred if not cleared). | Two. The product up close, label readable, held still. | Product close, label readable |

| 25–30s | The brief on a phone with one line highlighted. The creator reads it, then says it to camera. | Three. The approved claim, word for word. An edit cannot fix a paraphrase. | The claim, word for word |
| 30–35s | The empty room, camera still rolling, nobody speaking. | Four. Ten seconds of the room, nobody talking. It covers every cut. | Ten seconds of quiet room |
| 35–40s | A hand ticks four boxes on the shot list while the tripod is folded in the background. | Tick all four before the creator packs up. | Tick four before pack-up |
