"We already have an agency."
Founders often say it in the first ten minutes, and I take it as a reason to keep talking.

What I Heard in the Room, a pattern from this year.

The founder isn't asking me to replace anyone. They're telling me the work is covered and they don't want another vendor making more ads.

Fair. So I ask one question back.

When your agency changes, what does the brand keep?

Usually it's a shared drive full of files and a few people's instincts. The reasons one ad beat another tend to leave with the team that ran them.

That is the gap we built Galvor for. We sit alongside the agency, read every ad across 50+ attributes, and keep what worked and why in the brand's own memory.

The agency gets sharper briefs and a clear record of what to test next. The brand owns the learning, whoever runs the account next year.

A good agency is a reason to make its work count twice.
