Would you let an AI agent move 20% of your ad budget without your sign-off?

Most people answer this on instinct, as a question about the technology. I think the answer says more about the team than the tool.

To hand over a budget decision, you have to be able to state the rule behind it. When do we cut an ad. When do we put more money behind one.

A team that has those rules written down can give them to a new hire, an agency or an agent, and check the work afterwards. A team that carries them in two people's heads cannot hand them to anyone.

So before you vote, try writing your cut rule in one sentence. If that takes more than a minute, you have found something more useful than the poll result.
