Tomorrow your dashboards will fill up faster than on any other day this year.
None of them will tell you what to make on Monday.

Dashboards describe yesterday. A founder needs help deciding tomorrow.

Sale weekend is the most expensive data most D2C brands buy all year. Bigger budgets, more new creatives, more people refreshing the same screens.

Then on Monday someone asks what worked.

The answer usually comes back as a list of ad names with a ROAS next to each. That tells you which ads did well. It doesn't tell you why, so it can't tell you what to repeat in December.

That is how a brand pays for the same weekend twice. Once in spend, and again when the next sale starts from zero.

So before Friday begins, I would put one question on Monday's agenda and give it an owner:

What did this weekend teach us that we will use in the next brief?

If the answer is a list of ad names, keep asking.

Every ad should make the next one better. Sale weekend is when forgetting costs the most.
