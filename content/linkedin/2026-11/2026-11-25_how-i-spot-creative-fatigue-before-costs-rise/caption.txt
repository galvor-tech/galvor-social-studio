Tired ads and slow starters look the same on a cost chart.
One needs a new opening. The other needs you to leave it alone.

Both show a cost per order above your account average, so the cost column cannot separate them.

History can. An ad can only be tired if it was once good.

In Galvor's analysis of video ads launched May to September 2026, some ad styles started about 35% dearer per order in their first two months and ended close to average over their life. Cut those in week one and you never see the second half.
