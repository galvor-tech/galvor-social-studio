It's Black Friday. Somewhere, a brand just changed the offer on a video that's already been shot.
I've learnt to treat that moment as a new brief.

Sale weeks do this. A flat discount becomes buy one get one. The end date moves. A new hero product walks in.

The temptation is to send the creator a one-line WhatsApp and hope.

Here's what I do on day one.

Write down what changed in one line, and get the brand to reply "yes, that's it".

List every asset the change touches. Supers, captions, voiceover, end card, thumbnail.

Sort them into two piles: what the edit can fix, and what needs the creator again.

Give the creator a new due date before asking for the change. Nobody does good work against a deadline that quietly stayed the same.

Ask who approves the new version. Mid-change is exactly when a new reviewer appears.

It takes an hour. It saves the round where the old offer sneaks back into the final cut.
