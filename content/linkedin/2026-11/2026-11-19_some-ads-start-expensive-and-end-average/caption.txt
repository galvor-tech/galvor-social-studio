Some ads look like losers for two months, then end up average.
Cut them in week two and you never find out which kind you had.

We looked at every new video ad launched between May and September 2026 across the brands we work with. Some ad styles started about 35% dearer per order in their first two months. Over their whole life they ended close to average.

Before you cut an ad on early cost per order, check three things:

1. Have you run this style before? Look at how it behaved over its full run last time.
2. Is the ad new, or a copy of something already live?
3. Did you write down a review date before launch?

An early number is a first reading. Give the ad the time its style has needed before.
