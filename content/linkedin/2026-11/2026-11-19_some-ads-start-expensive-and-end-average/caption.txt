Some ads look like losers for two months, then end up average.
Cut them in week two and you never find out which kind you had.

We looked at every new video ad launched between May and September 2026 across the brands we work with. Some ad styles cost about 35% more per order in their first two months, then ended close to average over their full run.

The catch is that early on, a slow starter and a real loser look the same. Before you cut one, check:

1. Have you run this style before? See how it did over its whole run last time, not just the first weeks.
2. Is it actually new or close to an ad that's already live? A copy may never get going, so give the most time to ads that are really new.

A slow start doesn't always mean a bad ad.
