Six look-alike ads in three days: each got 48% less spend.
That is the festive-season habit most D2C teams are about to repeat next week.

Sale weeks push teams to launch more. More sizes, more cuts, more versions of the ad that worked in October.

Our analysis of new video ads launched from May to September 2026 says the extra versions pay back poorly.

New ads that looked like many ads already running got 36% less first-month spend.

When six or more look-alikes went live within three days, each one got 48% less.

Our reading: near-copies end up sharing one budget. You pay to make six ads and get the reach of far fewer.

A count you can do before Black Friday week:

Lay every planned ad side by side as thumbnails.
Group the ones a stranger would call the same ad.
Keep the strongest one from each group and hold the rest back.
Put the saved production time into one ad that looks different.

A short launch list where every ad has a reason to exist will teach you more than a full folder.
