The sale starts tomorrow. Decide today what it should teach you.
Most sale reports say what sold. Few say what the brand learned about its ads.

A sale is the biggest ad test most D2C brands run all year, and it usually runs without a question.

Pick one question before Black Friday goes live.

For example: at the same discount, does an ad that leads with the offer do better than an ad that leads with the product?

Then write three lines:

The question, in one sentence.
The two ads that answer it, identical except for the one thing you are testing.
What you will do in December if the first is ahead, and if the second is.

On Monday you will have revenue either way. These three lines add an answer you can brief from.
