Five things our ad data showed in 2026. Each with its number.
All five come from Galvor's analysis of ads launched from May to September across the brands we work with.

The short version: Meta's delivery now favours an ad that looks different from what you already run, and some ad styles need longer before you judge them.

Two limits. The analysis covers brands we work with, so treat each finding as a pattern to test in your own account. And four of the five are about spend and cost in the early weeks, which is delivery. Whether an ad sells over its life is a separate question.
