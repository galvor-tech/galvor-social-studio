Galvor doesn't wow every client in its first month yet.
I'd rather say that here than have someone find out in a pilot.

We ask brands to run a learning loop, so we run one on ourselves. Every fortnight we look at where clients got stuck.

The pattern I see most is the gap between a finding and an action.

A team reads that a certain opening costs more per order. They agree. Then the next brief looks exactly like the last one.

The finding was right. It arrived as a fact when the team needed a decision.

So we're changing three things.

Every finding now ends in a call: scale, cut, refresh or test.

Every finding says how sure we are, so a signal doesn't get treated like proof.

And the first fortnight ends with one brief written together, so the loop starts with something live.

It's unglamorous work. It is also the same thing we ask of every ad: make the next one better than the last.
