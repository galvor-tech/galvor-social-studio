The most expensive creative mistake is the one you pay for twice.
Once when you make it, and again when the next batch repeats it.

It usually starts with a win. One ad works, so the next briefs ask for more like it. A week later the account has several near-copies fighting for the same people.

Galvor looked at every new video ad launched May to September 2026 across the brands we work with.

New ads that looked like many running ads got 36% less first-month spend.

When six or more look-alikes went live within three days, each got 48% less.

The way out kept the part that sold. A proven message in a new look got 47% more first-month spend. A new message over a familiar first shot got 16% less.

For one brand, the creative diversity score went from 63 to 92 out of 100 two months after the changes we recommended.

Swipe for the pattern, the numbers and the one rule I'd give any team.
