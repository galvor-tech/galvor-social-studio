If you write briefs for creators, agencies or your in-house team, Shrutik's one-page template from this week is worth saving.

Nine fields. One page. Nothing a creator won't read.

The field we'd point to is the seventh: what the last campaign taught us. One line, taken from the data, written into every new brief. It's the difference between a campaign that starts from zero and one that starts ahead.

A few of the other fields that do a lot of work:
→ the moment: when the buyer would see this and what they're doing
→ the objection: what stops them, and how the ad answers it
→ done looks like: what we'll judge, and when

Shrutik is sending the editable version to anyone who comments "brief" on the original post, below.

A good brief removes ambiguity. It doesn't add pages.
