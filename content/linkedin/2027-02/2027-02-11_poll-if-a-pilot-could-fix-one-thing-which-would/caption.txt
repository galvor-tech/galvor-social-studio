If an AI pilot could fix one problem for your marketing team, which would you pick?
Only one. That's the hard part.

What I Heard in the Room: the pilots that stall usually tried to prove everything at once.

Ten features in the demo, eight goals in the plan, and at the end nobody can say what changed.

The pilots that go well start with one problem that already hurts. Someone on the team can name it, and finance would notice if it got better.

We would rather solve one painful problem than demo ten features. It makes the pilot shorter, the result clearer and the decision easier for everyone in the room, including the people who weren't at the demo.

So, one pick. Vote below.
