"We need more creators" is one of the most common asks I hear from D2C teams.
It's rarely the real problem.

What I Heard in the Room, a pattern from the last few months.

The brand has worked with dozens of creators. A few did well. Most didn't. The conclusion is that they need a bigger pool.

Usually they need a better filter.

When we launched Galvor Scout in November, we'd already run 8,673 creator checks. 66% of the creators we turned down had missed something the brand said was a must-have.

Those creators looked fine on reach and engagement. They just didn't fit the brief the brand had written.

More creators through the same filter means more of those misses, paid for one fee at a time.

So the first question I ask is about the brief. What does a creator have to be for this product, in plain words? Then we check every name against it.

A shorter list of the right people beats a long list of maybes.
