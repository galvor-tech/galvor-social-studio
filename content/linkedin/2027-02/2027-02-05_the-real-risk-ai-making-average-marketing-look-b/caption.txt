AI can make an average marketing team look very productive.

The output goes up. What the team knows about its buyers stays where it was.

This is the risk I would watch in 2027, more than any risk to jobs. If your weekly report counts creatives shipped, posts published and decks sent, AI will improve every line on it. The report will look better while the business stays the same.

The fix is one change to that report.

Remove the line that counts what was made. Add a line that counts questions answered.

A question is answered when a test ends in a decision: scale it, cut it, or change the brief. A test that ends in 'interesting' counts as zero.

Then read the number every fortnight. A team that ships forty ads and answers one question has had a slow fortnight. A team that ships eight and answers four has had a good one.

Output is what you paid for. A decision is what you got for the money.

Count the second one, and AI becomes useful for the right reason.
