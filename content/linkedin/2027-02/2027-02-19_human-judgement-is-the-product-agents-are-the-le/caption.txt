Agents are taking the work junior marketers used to learn on.

So judgement, which used to build up by itself, now has to be taught on purpose.

Pulling reports and tagging ads by hand was dull work. It was also how a new marketer saw hundreds of ads next to their numbers and slowly learned what good looks like. When an agent does that work, the senior people keep their judgement and the next group never forms theirs.

Two habits a team can start this week.

Call first, then look. Before anyone opens the agent's recommendation, each person writes their own call on the ad in one line: scale, cut or test, and why. Then compare. The gaps are the lesson.

Review one miss a fortnight. Take a decision that went wrong and trace what was known on the day it was made. The aim is to find the reasoning that failed, never to find a person to blame.

Both take under an hour, and neither needs a new tool.

Human judgement is the product. Agents are the leverage. Leverage on untrained judgement only gets a team to the wrong answer sooner.
