Your sales team is sitting on ad-training data.

Many of the accounts I've looked at optimise ads on what Meta can see: clicks, add-to-carts, purchases. For high-consideration products, a lot of the buying happens after that. A call, a WhatsApp chat, a demo, a second visit.

The people who know which leads were real sit in sales and support. They just never tell the ad system.

Three things from the sales side I'd want feeding the ads:
1. Which leads became customers. Not just how many leads came in.
2. Why the ones who didn't buy said no. Price? Wrong product? Not the person the ad promised?
3. What customers said in their own words on the call. Those lines often make better hooks than anything we'd write.

The first goes back to Meta as offline conversions, so it learns which people actually buy. The second and third go into the next brief.

When this loop is missing, the ads keep finding more people like the ones who fill in forms, not the ones who pay.

If your sales team keeps a lead-quality sheet that nobody in marketing opens, start there.
