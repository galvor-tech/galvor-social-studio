Worth a read before your next creative review: Raj on why three winning ads isn't a pattern yet.

It's an easy trap. The last three winners all had a founder on camera, so the team writes a rule: founder-led works for us.

Raj's four checks before an observation becomes a rule:

1. How many ads with the same trait lost?
2. What else did the winners share: offer, season, audience?
3. Did winners and losers get a fair share of budget?
4. Does it hold in the next cycle?

It's why we tag every attribute on every ad, winners and losers. You only see what the winners have when you can compare them with the ads that didn't make it.

Until then, it's a signal and a pattern to test, not a rule. Raj's full post is below.
