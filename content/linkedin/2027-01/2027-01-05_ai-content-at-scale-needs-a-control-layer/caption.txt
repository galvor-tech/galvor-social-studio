AI made it cheap to produce a hundred ads.
Checking them is still your job.

When making an ad costs almost nothing, the limit on a team moves from production to control. Without checks, a team launches many ads that look alike, and the ads compete with each other.

In Galvor's analysis of new video ads launched from May to September 2026, when six or more look-alikes launched within three days, each got 48% less first-month spend.

The slides have five checks to set up before the first batch of the year, and the one step most teams skip after the ads have run.

None of them needs new software. They need a rule and a name against it.
