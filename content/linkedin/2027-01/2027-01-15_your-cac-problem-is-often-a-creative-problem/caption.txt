Your CAC problem is often a creative problem that looks like a media problem.
January budget reviews are where that mix-up gets expensive.

When cost per order rises, the first fixes are usually media fixes. New audiences, new bid caps, a new agency brief on targeting.

Meta's own direction makes that a weaker bet each quarter. By Meta's public statements, its Andromeda system scores over a billion ads a second, and the ad itself now does much of the work of finding the right people.

So when an account fills up with ads that look alike, the system has fewer real choices to work with.

In Galvor's analysis of new video ads launched May to September 2026, new ads that looked like many ads already running got 36% less first-month spend.

On your dashboard that shows up as rising CAC. Someone suggests changing the targeting. The fix was sitting in the brief.

Before you change the agency or the audience plan this quarter, ask the team one question.

How many genuinely different ads did we launch last month?
