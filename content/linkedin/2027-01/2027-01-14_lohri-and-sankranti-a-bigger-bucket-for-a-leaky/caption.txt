Ad budgets work the same way, and January is when that is easiest to forget.

The new-year plan often sounds the same: last quarter worked, so scale spend.

Scaling before you fix how the team learns from creative is buying a bigger bucket for a leaky tap.

The leak is quiet. Ads that repeat what is already running. Lessons that live in one person's head. A winning ad nobody can explain, so nobody can repeat it.

In Galvor's analysis of ads launched May to September 2026, when six or more look-alike ads went live within three days, each got 48% less first-month spend. More money in, same leak.

Fixing the tap is less exciting. A brief that says what each ad is testing. A review every two weeks. A written note of what was learned, kept where the next person can find it.

Then scale. A harvest goes better when you know what you planted.
