During a sale, the discount is the one thing every ad shares.
So it is the weakest thing to open with.

Ad of the Week #4 is a pattern from sale season, shown on a made-up Republic Day ad for a cookware brand. No real brand and no real results.

The structure: open on the reason to want the product, show it doing its job, then land the offer as the reason to act today.

There is a delivery reason too. In Galvor's analysis of new video ads launched May to September 2026, six or more look-alikes launched within three days each got 48% less first-month spend. Sale week is when teams launch six versions of one banner.
