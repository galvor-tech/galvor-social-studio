Most creative learnings are one ad's result with a confident name.
“UGC works for us” often means one video did well in one week.

A finding from a single ad is worth keeping. It is also worth labelling honestly, because the next brief will be built on it.

Three labels cover it:

Proven. The same pattern showed up across several ads and more than one review cycle.
Signal. Seen once, or in a small sample. Worth a deliberate test.
Hunch. Someone's reading of the account, with no comparison behind it yet.

Then decide what each label is allowed to do.

A brief can lean on a proven finding without discussion. A signal gets one slot in the next batch, built to confirm it or end it. A hunch is written down with its owner's name, and is tested or dropped by the following review.

Nothing moves up a label because a senior person likes it.

After a few cycles the proven list will be short. That is normal, and it is the list to put real money behind.

The Republic Day sale has just handed you a pile of single results. Label them before you brief February.
