Three things we decided Galvor would not do.
Tomorrow is National Startup Day, and what a company refuses to do describes it better than a feature list.

We will not touch your media budget. Our work is the creative that goes into it, and advice is easier to trust when the adviser does not control the spend.

We will not give an ad a single score. An ad is a hook, a cast, a pace, a setting and a call to action. We price each attribute against cost per order, because briefs are written attribute by attribute.

We will not hide the reasoning. Vira, our digital co-worker, shows its thinking, so a marketer can disagree with a single step.

To founders building from India today: write down what your company will not do. It explains you faster than a tagline.
