Making ads got cheap.
Knowing which ad to make did not.

Those are two different uses of AI, and brand teams tend to buy the first one.

AI-generated creative means using a tool to produce more ads, faster. AI-informed creative means using it to read what your past ads taught you, and then writing the brief.

The second is the better bet, and here is the evidence I would put in front of a founder.

In Galvor's analysis of new video ads launched from May to September 2026, a proven message shown in a new look got 47% more first-month spend. A new message over a familiar first shot got less than average.

So the most valuable brief is short: keep this message, change how it looks. You can only write it if you know which of your messages is proven.

A check for your next batch. For every ad, fill in two blanks. The message is kept from which earlier ad? What will look different?

If the first blank stays empty, you are generating blind, whichever tool you use.

Decide what to make first. After that, the tool matters much less.
