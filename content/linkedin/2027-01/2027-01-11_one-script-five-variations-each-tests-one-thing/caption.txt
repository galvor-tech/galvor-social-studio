One script, five variations. Each variation changes exactly one thing.

The easy way to brief variations is to change everything at once: new opening, new creator, new music, new ask. Five ads come back, one does better, and nobody can say which change did it.

Keep the script fixed. Change one of five things per variation: the opening, the person, the setting, the format or the ask. Name each variation in the brief and use the same name on the file and the ad.

One caution. The variations have to look different at a glance, or they compete with each other.

The next step is to put these on a calendar. Decoded #11 shows how.
