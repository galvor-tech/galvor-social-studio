AI creative needs a few rounds. Say so on the first call.
A client who expects a finished film in round one will read every later round as a failure.

With the first briefs of the year going out this week, here is how I set it up on that call. Each round gets a name and one job.

Round one is direction. One question only: is this the right idea, look and voice? Small flaws are expected here and nobody lists them.

Round two is accuracy. Product, pack, claim and on-screen text.

Round three is polish. Timing, sound and subtitles.

Then the rule that protects the dates. A note belongs to its round. A change of idea in round three sends us back to round one, and the go-live date moves with it. Said on day one, that is a plan. Said in week three, it sounds like an excuse.

After the call, it goes in writing: the rounds, what each is for, and who replies by when.

This matters more with AI because round one arrives so fast. People then expect the final cut at the same speed.

Rounds stop feeling like rework when everyone was told they were coming.
