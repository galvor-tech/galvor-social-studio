In a gifting ad, the buyer and the user are different people.
That changes who the first three seconds should talk to.

Ad of the Week #3 is a pattern, shown on a made-up example: a gifting ad for a candle brand. No real brand and no real results.

A normal product ad answers 'do I want this?'. A gifting ad has to answer the giver's question: 'will they like it, and will it look like I thought about it?'

So the opening names the person receiving the gift. The product arrives as the answer. The giver's worries get handled before the price.

Valentine's briefs get written this month. Swipe for the structure, and one caution from our data.
