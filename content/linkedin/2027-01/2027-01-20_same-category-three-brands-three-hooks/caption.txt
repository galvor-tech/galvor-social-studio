Line up three ads from your category. Read only the openings.
What they share tells you where your next ad will blend in.

This is a 20-minute exercise with the public Meta Ad Library. I have shown it on a made-up set of three sunscreen ads, so no real brand is being judged.

Sort each opening into one of three lanes. It opens on a problem, on proof, or on a person. Then count.

One caution on what the count means. The library shows what a brand is running. It does not show what is selling. So a crowded lane is a fact about the feed.

The empty lane is a test worth running on your own ads.
