Three numbers I'd keep on a founder's marketing dashboard.
The rest can live with the team.

A founder's page should answer one question: is our marketing getting smarter, or only bigger?

Most founder dashboards answer a different one. They show what happened last week, ad by ad, in more columns than anyone reads on a Monday.

The three I'd keep:

Cost per order by idea. Which openings, faces and messages bring orders cheaply, across all the ads that use them.

Share of spend on new ideas. Too low and you stop learning. Too high and you never cash in on what you know.

Learnings used. How many findings from the last fortnight made it into the next brief.

CTR, CPM and the ad-by-ad ROAS ranking still matter. They belong in the team's review, where someone can act on them that day.
