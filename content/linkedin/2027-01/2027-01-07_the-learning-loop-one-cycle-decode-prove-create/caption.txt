Every two weeks, our loop asks one question of every ad that ran: what should the next ad keep?
Here is one cycle, start to finish.

Decode. Every ad gets tagged on 50+ attributes: hook, cast, pacing, setting, CTA.

Prove. Each attribute is priced against cost per order. A winning ad becomes a list of reasons you can brief again.

Create. The next briefs start from those reasons. Keep what earned its place. Change what didn't.

Run. Scale, cut, shift. We move creative and never touch the media budget.

Then the next cycle reads what the last one changed, and the brand's memory grows by one more round.

In one Run cycle (Cycle 14), cost per order came down 18%. A single cycle is a signal. Twenty-six of them a year is a habit.
