Six questions to ask before you connect your ad account to anything.
It is Data Privacy Day, and most D2C teams grant access in one click, mid-demo.

Your ad account holds your spend, your creative and what your customers respond to. Any tool that reads it, ours included, should answer these in writing:

1. Do you need read access or manage access, and why?
2. Who inside your company can see our data?
3. Is our data used to train models or build benchmarks that other brands see?
4. If it is pooled, how is it anonymised?
5. What happens to our data when we leave, and how soon?
6. Can we see a log of what was accessed?

A good vendor has the answers ready. A slow or vague reply is an answer too.
