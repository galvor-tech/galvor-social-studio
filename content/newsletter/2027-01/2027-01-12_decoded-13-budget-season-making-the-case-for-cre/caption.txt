Media spend gets approved in five minutes. Creative spend gets twenty minutes of questions.

The reason is simple: finance funds what it can measure, and creative usually arrives without a measure.

In Issue #9 of Decoded, Raj and Sunil show how to make the case for creative in the language finance already uses: cost per order by attribute, spend that taught you nothing, and time to a new winner. Plus a five-step plan to ask for a learning budget instead of a production budget, ahead of the new financial year.
