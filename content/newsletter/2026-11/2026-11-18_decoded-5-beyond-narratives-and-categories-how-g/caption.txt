Your best ad has a story. That isn't why it worked.

In Issue #4 of Decoded, Raj breaks a winning ad into the six parts that actually carry a result: the hook, the cast, the setting, the pacing, the proof and the CTA.

When teams copy a winning story, they often copy everything except the part that worked. In the ads Galvor analysed, a new message over a familiar first shot got 16% less first-month spend. A proven message in a new look got 47% more.

Inside: the one-page method to read your own winners, and five moves for your next brief.
