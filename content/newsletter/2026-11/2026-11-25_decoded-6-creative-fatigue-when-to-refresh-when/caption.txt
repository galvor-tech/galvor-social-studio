"It's fatigued" is the most common reason a good ad gets switched off. It's often the wrong diagnosis.

In Issue #5 of Decoded, Raj separates three kinds of fatigue that look identical in a dashboard: a tired opening, a saturated audience and a reason to buy that has stopped working. Only one of them means cut.

Also inside: why look-alike refreshes backfire (in Galvor's data, six or more look-alikes launched within three days each got 48% less first-month spend) and a ten-minute check to run before anyone hits pause.
