Most 2027 plans landing this week answer one question: what will we post?

Very few answer the question that matters more: what will we know by March that we don't know now?

In Issue #7 of Decoded, Raj makes the case for a test calendar instead of a content calendar: one question per fortnight, a fixed test budget, the expected result written before launch, and India's 2027 festival and sale dates mapped to the reasons to buy worth testing. It includes a one-page template you can copy into your planning doc.
